About & downloads

A proposed national cycling route network connecting Victoria BC to Cape Spear NL, with connector, access, and ferry routes — plus campgrounds, bike shops, and services along the way. Built and maintained by Sam Vekemans.

Downloads (full-resolution originals):

  • KMZ — for Organic Maps / CoMaps / Google Earth
  • GPX — for GPS units & route apps (large)
  • GDB — Garmin BaseCamp, with POI folders
  • ZIP — POI data bundle
  • RideWithGPS collection
