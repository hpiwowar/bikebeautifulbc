Add Stops to Your Route

Riding a route you already have? Choose its GPX file and we'll find the stops near your line: cafés, groceries, campgrounds, bike shops, places to sleep. They come from BC Bike Stops, our directory of cyclist-welcoming places across BC. Pick the ones you want, then download your route back with those stops built in, as waypoints your GPS shows along the way.

Your file never leaves your computer. This whole page runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and we never see your route.
or see how it works with a sample route: a day on the Sea to Sky Trail, Whistler down to Squamish.
