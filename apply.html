Join We Welcome Cyclists

BC's cycle tourism accreditation program. Free to join. Accredited businesses get a full listing in our searchable directory with the green Accredited badge, highlighted placement in route-planning tools, and features in our newsletter.

The form takes about 10 minutes. We review applications ourselves and aim to respond within two weeks.

What you get

  • Full listing in our directory with the green Accredited badge
  • Highlighted placement in route-planning tools
  • Features in our newsletter and social channels
  • A digital "We Welcome Cyclists" badge for your website
