Join BC Bike Stops

BC's cycle tourism accreditation program. Free to join.

Cycle tourists plan their trips around businesses that genuinely welcome them. In Quebec, where the Bienvenue cyclistes! program has run for decades, 31% of overnight cycle tourists adapt their itinerary based on certification (47% stayed at a certified establishment, and two-thirds of those said certification influenced their choice). The Accredited badge is how we signal that to BC's growing cycle tourism market.

What Accreditation brings

  • Full and customized listing in our BC Bike Stops directory with the teal Accredited badge
  • Highlighted placement in route-planning tools
  • Features in our newsletter and social channels
  • A digital "BC Bike Stops" badge for your website, if you would like that

The form takes about 10 minutes. We review applications ourselves and aim to respond within two weeks. Accreditation lasts two years — we check in then to confirm your listing is still accurate.
