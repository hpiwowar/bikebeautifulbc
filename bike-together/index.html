Bike together

Not every BC trip has to be planned solo. Companies run guided and self-guided bike tours here, and a few community groups would love to have you along for a ride.

Guided tours#

A guide travels with you, whether you're sleeping in hotels or camping with a support van.

  • Great Explorations · Gulf Islands, BC Coast Mountains, and the Kettle Valley
  • Into The Wild · Vancouver Island, Okanagan, Kootenays, and multi-day BC routes
  • Freewheeling Adventures · Okanagan and the Kettle Valley Rail Trail
  • Bicycle Adventures · Selkirk and Kootenay tour
  • VBT Cycling Vacations · Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands
  • Backroads · Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands
  • Vélo Québec Voyages · Okanagan Valley, accompanied option
  • Sockeye Cycle · Haines-based outfitter; the Golden Circle through Alaska, the Yukon, and BC’s far northwest corner
  • Adventure Cycling Association (ACA) · a US-based nonprofit whose van-supported and self-contained camping tours occasionally cross into BC

Self-guided tours#

Nobody rides with you, but the route, the beds, and your luggage are all arranged.

  • Into The Wild · self-guided option
  • Randonnée Tours · Gulf Islands, 5 or 7 days
  • KVR Outfitters · Kettle Valley Rail Trail
  • Great Canadian Trails · Kettle Valley Rail Trail
  • Vélo Québec Voyages · Okanagan Valley, self-guided option
  • Sockeye Cycle · Golden Circle rental and logistics package, touring bike and camping gear included

Ride with others#

Prefer company to logistics? These groups organize rides and trips anyone can join.

  • Gravel Buddies · group rides for women, gender-diverse, and 2SLGBTQIA+ riders, based in Vancouver
  • Van Island Bikepack Collective · group bikepacking trips and campouts on Vancouver Island
  • Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · a long-running BC touring club, day rides and multi-day tours
