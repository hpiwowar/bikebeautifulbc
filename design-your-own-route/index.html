Design your own route

Some of the best BC trips start with a blank map. If you love plotting your own line, you're in good company. Tools like Ride with GPS let you draw a route, add cues, and send it straight to your bike computer or phone.

Find places to stop#

A line on a map becomes a trip when you know where you'll sleep, eat, and refill.

Our BC Bike Stops directory tracks thousands of places to sleep, eat, refill water, and explore, the kind of details that turn a line on a map into a real trip.

Something better is coming#

We're building a free, BC-made route designer that knows the rail trails, the backroads, the ferries, and the services. Whether that means a gentle e-bike ride on quiet paths, a long day on pavement, or a week deep in the backcountry, dream it up and we'll help you make it real.
