Donation

The BC Cycle Tourism Society is a BC nonprofit society.

Donations are much appreciated and support cycle tourism development across British Columbia.

Because our work is primarily tourism development rather than a recognized charitable purpose under Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) rules, we are not a registered charity and cannot issue tax receipts. If you wish to support cycling in BC and receive a tax receipt, check out our Founding Partner organization charities:

  • BC Cycling Coalition
  • Trails BC
