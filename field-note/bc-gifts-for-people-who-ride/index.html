BC gifts for people who ride (and where they're really made)

November 27, 2026

This month I went looking for BC-made gifts for people who ride bikes. Here are about 30 fun ones, including a tee with a Sasquatch on a bike, a bear spray holder made by one person in Fernie, and coffee from a Victoria roaster that delivers by bike.

Most of the bike gear that's truly made in BC comes from tiny shops, often just one or two people.

Every pick says where it's made. "Made in BC" means the thing itself is built here. "Printed in BC" and "Roasted in BC" mean that step happens here. Where the maker doesn't say, I say that too.

Prices are in Canadian dollars, as listed on November 20.

Last chance: Two Wheel Gear, Vancouver. The company behind the pannier that turns into a backpack is closing on December 31, after more than 20 years. The panniers still in stock are $179.99 to $199.99 in the closing sale. BC company, made-in not stated.

Under $10#

Myra-Bellevue Park sticker, BC Parks Foundation, $6. A park scene with an enthusiastic cyclist in it. Myra Canyon is where the Kettle Valley Railway (KVR) trestles are, so give it to anyone who rode them this summer. All the profits go to BC's parks. Made in Canada.

Myra-Bellevue Park keychain, BC Parks Foundation, $8.99. The same park scene as the sticker, on a metal keychain. All the profits go to BC's parks. Made-in not stated.

Bike World sticker, Kindred Coast, Vancouver Island, $6. A small vinyl sticker from an Island studio. Printed in Canada.

"Bike Van Isle" sticker, Bough & Antler, Campbell River, $5. A waterproof sticker for the bottle or the frame of anyone who has toured Vancouver Island, or plans to. Made in Canada.

Tandem Bike Party card, Porchlight Press, Vancouver, $6.50. A letterpress card, blank inside, for the person you ride with. Made in BC.

Instant coffee pouch, Lark Coffee Roasters, Creston, $3.50. One cup of coffee that weighs four grams, which matters when you're carrying everything you own up a hill. Four pouches are $13. Roasted in BC, freeze-dried in Alberta.

$10 to $25#

Let's Go Biking! guidebooks, $19.95 to $24.95 in paperback, $14.95 as ebooks. Colleen MacDonald's three guidebooks cover rides around Vancouver, on the Islands and Sunshine Coast, and from Hope to Cranbrook. That last one, Okanagan and Beyond, has more than 140 routes. There's a $15 neckwarmer too, and a portion of every sale goes to BC trail advocacy groups. I should tell you that Colleen is a director of the BC Cycle Tourism Society, which publishes this site. Written in BC.

Eleven Speed Coffee, Victoria, $24.75 for a 340 g bag of the Podium espresso roast. A roaster run by bike people. They deliver by bike and host group rides. Roasted in BC.

Freeze-dried meals, West Coast Kitchen, Cowichan Valley, $17.95 to $21.95. Camp dinners from a family farm, like butter chicken with rice. They're 15% off until December 3. Made in BC.

South Away: The Pacific Coast on Two Wheels, by Meaghan Marie Hackinen, $20.95. A touring memoir that starts in Terrace and heads down the coast to Baja. It's the only gift on this list with a link to northern BC. I couldn't find a single bike gear maker up north. Written in BC, published in Alberta.

Cycling the Islands, by John Crouch, Rocky Mountain Books, Victoria, $25. The updated edition, with more than 30 rides on 11 islands, including Salt Spring, Pender, Mayne, Galiano, Gabriola, Denman, and Hornby. Published in BC.

Fading Light socks, 7mesh, Squamish, $20. Some colours are on sale from $12. 7mesh is open about sewing its clothing overseas, but this sock's page doesn't say where. BC brand, made-in not stated.

"I Stop For Slugs" bumper sticker, Owl & Bear Studio, $11. Not strictly a bike gift, but it's funny. The maker suggests your bumper, your water bottle, or your bike. I found it at Bough & Antler in Campbell River. Made-in not stated.

$25 to $50#

BIKE eco tee, Kindred Coast, Vancouver Island, $49.50. The bike on the front is drawn full of mountains, waves, trees, and a tent. The fabric is knit, cut, sewn, and screen-printed in Canada. Made in Canada.

Speedy Bike tee, Ole Originals, Vancouver, $39. Printed by hand, one at a time, on a manual press in a small Vancouver studio. It comes in women's sizes too. Printed in BC.

Biking Squatch tee, Westcoastees, Cowichan Valley, $35. A Sasquatch on a bike, letting the breeze through his fur. There's a kids' version for $27. Printed in BC.

Merino cycling socks, Samsara Cycle, Vancouver, $35. From a company that designs cycling clothes for women, in Vancouver. They're on sale for $29. BC brand, made-in not stated.

Classic Saddle Roll, Farsik Supply, Victoria, $28. A small roll for a tube and tools, in eight colours, handmade in the Victoria area. Made in BC.

Bear spray holder, Ghostrider Equipment, Fernie, $50. It holds bear spray on your bike where you can reach it. One person designs, prototypes, and builds these in the Kootenays. If someone you love tours in bear country, this is the one. Made in BC.

OSnack variety pack, Hornby Organic, Comox, $26.98. Twelve small organic bars from a family company that started on Hornby Island. They're an official supplier to Cycling Canada. Made in BC.

McCulloch's Wonder, by Barrie Sanford, $27.95. The story of how the Kettle Valley Railway got built, for anyone who has ridden the trail and wondered who thought a railway there was a good idea. A BC story.

Cycling Vancouver Island, by John Crouch, Rocky Mountain Books, Victoria, $30. More than 40 routes. This is the 2022 edition. An updated one was due this year and has slipped to April 2027. Published in BC.

$50 to $100#

Top-tube pack, mini, Shaka Packs, Kelowna, $90. A little bag for snacks and a phone, from the only bike bag maker I found in the Okanagan. Each one is built to order, so order early. Made in BC.

Cargo Cage, Wildwood Cycles, Nanaimo, $55. A cage for strapping a dry bag or a big bottle to your fork, from a solo builder who has been making frames since 2013. Made in BC.

Hippie hip pack, Northern Ultralight, Nelson, $80. Designed and made in a small workshop in Nelson. Made in BC.

XL Cargo Cage, Fauna Components, Vancouver, $79 or $85. A stainless steel cage, new this fall, from a woman-owned company. It's $79 in silver and $85 in black. Made in BC, with some imported parts.

$100 to $300#

Candy Pack, HMPL Design, Vancouver, $105. Sewn to order in a one-person Chinatown studio, in 16 fabrics and colours. Made in BC.

Little Basket, Threefold Objects, Vancouver, $105. A stainless steel front basket that's fabricated in Abbotsford, then welded and finished in Vancouver. Made in BC, with some imported parts.

Bar Bag Pro, Farsik Supply, Victoria, $125. The big sibling of the saddle roll above. Made in BC.

GR1 front light, Full On Lighting, Victoria, $149. I didn't find another bike-light company in BC. This one says the light is designed and tested in coastal BC. It doesn't say where it's made, so I can't either. Designed in BC, made-in not stated.

Gift cards#

If you'd rather let them choose: WZRD Bikes on Vancouver Island ($25 to $300), HMPL Design ($25 to $500), Hornby Organic ($25 to $100), and Eleven Speed Coffee ($20 to $100).

For the rider who has everything#

And for the rider who has everything? It's hard to go wrong with a donation in their name.

Our Community Bikes in Vancouver gives refurbished bikes and free repairs to people facing financial barriers. $280 covers a refurbished kids' bike. $160 covers a year of repairs for one person. You can give in someone's honour, and they'll get an e-card. Gifts made now are being matched. Your own town may have a community bike shop doing the same kind of work.

Or give to the groups that look after the trails they ride. GivingTuesday is December 1. That same $160 pays to surface one metre of the Shuswap North Okanagan Rail Trail. A lifetime membership in Island Pathways is $10. Our Trail Organizations page lists the groups that look after BC's touring trails, with links to donate, and Who to thank for our BC trails goes through them trail by trail.

Happy holidays, and happy riding.

Prices and stock checked November 20, 2026. No affiliate links, no paid placements.
