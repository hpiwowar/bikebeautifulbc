What you're looking at#

Dark blue rides are Rouvy's own routes. Orange rides were filmed by riders with a GoPro and added through Rouvy's Route Creator. In the app, rider-filmed rides stay hidden until you switch on "Include community routes".

The two rides with a ring around the dot are rail trails: the Myra Canyon trestles on the Kettle Valley Rail Trail, and the Okanagan Rail Trail from Coldstream to Lake Country. Every other ride here is on a road, and most of Rouvy's own are on a highway.

Opening a ride#

Rouvy's own routes each have a public page, so those links open for anyone. Rider-filmed rides only open if you're signed in to Rouvy.

Where the numbers come from#

We read the list from Rouvy's rider map on November 21, 2026, and kept every ride that starts in British Columbia and has video. Distance, climbing, and maximum grade are Rouvy's figures. We leave maximum grade off the rider-filmed rides, because it comes from a camera's GPS and one bad reading can become the maximum.

The lines are Rouvy's own, simplified. "Where it runs" is our reading of each ride's name and line, and we haven't checked every one.
