BC Cycle Tourism Society, https://bccycletourism.ca/research/trans-canada-trail-comfort/
From the cycling sections on tctrail.ca, retrieved 18 July 2026. Built 2026-10-21.

Each name is the trail section's name on tctrail.ca, with its tctrail.ca ID in brackets.
Many are named for the city or regional district that looks after them ("City of Burnaby",
"Tri-Cities"), not for a signed trail. "On the ground" lists the OpenStreetMap names of
the paths and roads each section follows, longest first (unnamed ways left out).

Lengths in km:
  matched  the length we matched to OpenStreetMap and rated (the numbers on the page)
  closed   Temporarily Closed on tctrail.ca, drawn but not rated
  stated   tctrail.ca's own length for the whole section
