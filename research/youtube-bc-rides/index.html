What you're looking at#

Each video is a bike ride somewhere in British Columbia, filmed from the rider's point of view. Put one on while you pedal a trainer or a spin bike and you can ride the seawall in January. "One continuous ride" means the video follows a single ride from start to finish, sometimes sped up. "Edited" means it's cut together, with highlights, stops, drone shots, or someone talking to the camera.

Dots on the map are where each ride starts, as near as we could tell. Some are only placed at the town, so a dot in the middle of a city may be a few kilometres off.

How we found them#

We ran 244 YouTube searches on November 21, 2026, mostly place names paired with words like "bike ride POV" and "virtual cycling". That turned up 3,625 videos. We kept the ones that are real rides in BC, using AI to read each title and description. Nobody has watched all of them.

So the tags, the surface, the sound, and "where it runs" are our best reading of what each creator wrote, not a review. Some will be off. View counts are from the day we looked. Videos get taken down, so a few links will stop working over time.

Most of what's here is in Metro Vancouver and around Victoria. That's where people film. The Interior and the North are thin, and if you know a good one we missed, we'd like to hear about it.

For creators#

Thank you for filming these. If you made one of the videos here and would rather it wasn't listed, or you've filmed a BC ride we don't have, email hello@bccycletourism.ca. We aren't affiliated with YouTube or with any of the creators.
