Elbow Valley Cycle Club

cycling club visit →

Elbow Valley Cycle Club is a Calgary-based recreational cycling club, one of the largest in Western Canada with roughly 700 members. It organizes about 250 events a year, including road, gravel, and multi-day touring rides, several of which cross into British Columbia.

Their BC collection

Elbow Valley Cycle Club's BC routes span road touring in the Kootenay Rockies, a long-distance gravel route through the Thompson-Okanagan, Kootenay Rockies, and Vancouver Coast and Mountains regions, and shorter day rides on Vancouver Island and in the Thompson-Okanagan. Most routes are paved road rides in the 30-120 km range, alongside one multi-day gravel route exceeding 1,300 km with 67% unpaved surface. Terrain ranges from rolling valley roads to sustained mountain climbing, including sections of the club's flagship Golden Triangle tour through the Rockies.

Van to Can Day 1
115 km · 811 m up
AmbitiousShares roads with trafficFast-road sections · 9 kmMostly pavedSome climbing

The opening day of the Elbow Valley Cycle Club's Vancouver-to-Canmore tour: 115 km of mostly paved, mostly flat riding east out of the city through the Fraser Valley, with about 800 m of gentle climbing. Straightforward touring with traffic for company.

Vancouver Island · 1

Photo: Peter O HaraComox, BC
Campbell River
33 km · 147 m up
EasyShares roads with trafficFast-road sections · 10 kmMostly pavedGentle grades

An easy 33 km rolling ride around Campbell River, taking in the oceanfront and the town's edges with barely 150 m of climbing. Campbell River calls itself the salmon capital of the world, and the seawalk's fishing pier makes a fitting stop.

Thompson Okanagan · 2

Photo: Ryan FlettCastlegar, BC
BC Gravel (BC Only)
1300 km · 17063 m up
EpicSome traffic sharingFast-road sections · 177 kmMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

BC Gravel crosses about 1,300 km of southern British Columbia, from the Coast Mountains through the Okanagan to the Kootenays, roughly two-thirds unpaved with over 17,000 m of climbing. A multi-week undertaking on quiet backroads, best planned with the same care as any long bikepacking traverse.

Kootenay Rockies · 2

Photo: Mitch WintonGolden, BC
Golden Triangle Day 2
107 km · 635 m up
AmbitiousShares roads with trafficMostly fast roadsMostly pavedGentle grades

The middle day of the classic Golden Triangle road tour: 107 km of pavement from Radium Hot Springs to Golden through the Columbia River Valley, home to North America's longest contiguous wetland. Rolling rather than mountainous, with a new shoulder added in both directions in 2023. Highway riding, so expect traffic.

Golden Triangle Day 3
87 km · 1546 m up
AmbitiousShares roads with trafficMostly fast roadsMostly pavedLots of climbingRemote stretches

The climbing day of the Golden Triangle tour: 87 km from Golden up through Yoho National Park past Field to Lake Louise, gaining about 1,500 m along the way. All paved, all highway, and all Rockies: this is the leg the tour is remembered for. Expect traffic on Highway 1.
