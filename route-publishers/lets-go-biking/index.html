Let's Go Biking

guidebook author Visit Let's Go Biking ↗

Let's Go Biking is a British Columbia cycling guidebook series written by Colleen MacDonald, a champion of slow, scenic, family-friendly riding. Her three guidebooks — Around Vancouver; Okanagan and Beyond; and Vancouver Island, the Gulf Islands and Sunshine Coast — come with a companion website of mapped rides, walks, and runs.

Their BC collection

Let's Go Biking's BC coverage spans all three guidebooks, with 179 mapped rides in this collection. Most are day rides on quiet roads, greenways, dykes, and rail trails, rated easy to moderate, alongside longer options up to multi-day Kettle Valley Rail Trail (KVR) trips. The focus is family-friendly, scenery-first cycling rather than road touring or bikepacking.
