How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum, plus some climbing.

The fifth day of a Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society multi-day tour, riding 110 km in the Nanaimo area with about 1,100 m of rolling climbing, nearly all paved. Club-style road touring: steady pace, shared roads, coffee halfway.

Fast-road stretches#

We believe these are the fast, unprotected roads on this route. We can tell you which roads and roughly how far, but not the order you will ride them. If you see an error, tell us and we'll fix it.

  • about 25 km of West Island Highway · near Dashwood
  • about 12 km of South Island Highway · near Fanny Bay
  • about 8 km of Northwest Bay Road · near Bryn
  • about 6 km of East Island Highway · near Nanoose Bay
  • short stretches on other roads · 3 km total

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, ɬaʔamɩn gɩǰɛ (Tla’amin), Snaw-naw-as, Snuneymuxw, Te’mexw Treaty Association, We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
