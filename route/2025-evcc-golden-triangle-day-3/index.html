How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing.

The climbing day of the Golden Triangle tour: 87 km from Golden up through Yoho National Park past Field to Lake Louise, gaining about 1,500 m along the way. All paved, all highway, and all Rockies: this is the leg the tour is remembered for. Expect traffic on Highway 1.

Traditional territories#

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Michif Piyii (Métis), Niitsítpiis-stahkoii ᖹᐟᒧᐧᐨᑯᐧ ᓴᐦᖾᐟ (Blackfoot / Niitsítapi ᖹᐟᒧᐧᒣᑯ), Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), and Tsuut’ina.
