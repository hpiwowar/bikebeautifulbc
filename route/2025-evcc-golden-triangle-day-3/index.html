Golden Triangle Day 3

A ride mapped and described by Elbow Valley Cycle Club

75 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

87 km · Full dayAmbitiousRide with trafficMostly pavedLots of climbingRemote stretches

The climbing day of the Golden Triangle tour: 87 km from Golden up through Yoho National Park past Field to Lake Louise, gaining about 1,500 m along the way. All paved, all highway, and all Rockies: this is the leg the tour is remembered for. Expect traffic on Highway 1.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Elbow Valley Cycle Club.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
