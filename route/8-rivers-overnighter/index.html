8 Rivers Overnighter

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

3 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

206 km · Weekend or moreChallengingCalm streets, some sharingAlmost all gravelChunky gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

Tucked into the East Kootenays in Ktunaxa and Secwepemc territory, this 206 km loop links one spectacular river valley after another on wide gravel roads and sneaky little connectors, with a dip into Nipika's singletrack partway. The last big pass is the unanimous highlight: wildflowers, meadows, and Rockies views both directions, then hours through the staggering regrowth of old wildfires. Two to three days with creek crossings, and rustic Lussier Hot Springs waits near the start for afterwards.
