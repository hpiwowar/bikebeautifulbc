Alberni Bam Bam, Vancouver Island

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

153 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

507 km · ExpeditionAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

A week-long, roughly 500 km loop from Nanaimo around the south island's wild interior: gravel and rough doubletrack through the Cowichan Valley to Port Renfrew, past the Walbran Valley and Fairy Creek rainforests at the heart of BC's old-growth story, then to Bamfield, where the MV Frances Barkley, a 65-year-old heritage ship, carries you four hours up the Alberni Inlet. Swim spots and quirky communities all the way; long stretches without resupply, so plan carefully. Designed to be ridden car-free from the Departure Bay ferry.
