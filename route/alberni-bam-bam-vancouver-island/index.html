How we describe routes →

“The Alberni Bam Bam forms a loop on the south end of Vancouver Island via forest service roads, old access roads, and occasional stretches of doubletrack. The route prioritizes scenic gravel roads and stunning swimming spots in lakes, rivers, and the ocean. It passes by rainforests that have been at the forefront of the fight against old-growth logging: the Central Walbran Valley and Fairy Creek.”

— BIKEPACKING.com
