Barry's Loop

A ride mapped and described by Bike Gear Database

235 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

58 km · 618 m up · Full day

AmbitiousLow-traffic ridingMixed surfaceChunky gravelSome climbing

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of some loose surface + some climbing.

A 58 km mixed-surface loop out of Esquimalt near Victoria, about 40% unpaved with 600 m of climbing and some genuinely rough stretches. A local's line through the South Island's backroads, named for the rider who shared it.
