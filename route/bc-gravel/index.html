BC Gravel (BC Only)

A ride mapped and described by Elbow Valley Cycle Club

1,462 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

1300 km · ExpeditionEpicCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

BC Gravel crosses about 1,300 km of southern British Columbia, from the Coast Mountains through the Okanagan to the Kootenays, roughly two-thirds unpaved with over 17,000 m of climbing. A multi-week undertaking on quiet backroads, best planned with the same care as any long bikepacking traverse.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Elbow Valley Cycle Club.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
