How we describe routes →

The first half of the Great Northern Bikepacking Route's British Columbia section: about 583 km from Richmond through the Coast Mountains into the Interior, on mixed pavement and gravel. The climbing is the story, nearly 12,000 m of it, as the route fights its way up from sea level through some of the steepest country in the province. A committed multi-day leg of a much longer journey. Route created by Matt Kadey.

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Dënéndeh, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Lil’wat, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), St̓át̓imc Tmicw (St’at’imc), Stz’uminus, šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam), Syilx (Okanagan), and Tŝilhqot’in Nen.
