BC Part Two (BC Only)

A ride mapped and described by Great Northern Bikepacking Route

243 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

1187 km · ExpeditionEpicCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

The second half of the Great Northern Bikepacking Route's British Columbia section: nearly 1,200 km through the Kootenay Rockies and Thompson Okanagan, mostly unpaved, with an enormous amount of climbing, over 18,000 m in total. A committed multi-week undertaking through big, quiet country.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Great Northern Bikepacking Route.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
