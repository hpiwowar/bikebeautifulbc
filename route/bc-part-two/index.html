Great Northern Bikepacking Route, BC Part Two

A ride described at Great Northern Bikepacking Route

1187 km · 18,412 m up · Expedition · Kootenay Rockies

EpicSome traffic sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

Difficulty: Epic, because of sheer distance, plus lots of climbing.

The second half of the Great Northern Bikepacking Route's British Columbia section: nearly 1,200 km through the Kootenay Rockies and Thompson Okanagan, mostly unpaved, with an enormous amount of climbing, over 18,000 m in total. A committed multi-week undertaking through big, quiet country. Route created by Matt Kadey.

Traditional territories#

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Michif Piyii (Métis), Niitsítpiis-stahkoii ᖹᐟᒧᐧᐨᑯᐧ ᓴᐦᖾᐟ (Blackfoot / Niitsítapi ᖹᐟᒧᐧᒣᑯ), Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), Syilx (Okanagan), and Tsuut’ina.
