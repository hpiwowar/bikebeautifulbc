Iron Mine Bay Grando

A ride mapped and described by British Columbia Randonneurs

162 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

102 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelSome climbing

A 102 km populaire from Saanich in the BC Randonneurs tradition, rolling out toward Iron Mine Bay on a near-even mix of pavement and gravel with about 770 m of climbing. Sociable, non-competitive distance riding; the route is there year-round even when the club event is not.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on British Columbia Randonneurs.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
