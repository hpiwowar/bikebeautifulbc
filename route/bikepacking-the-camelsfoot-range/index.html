Bikepacking the Camelsfoot Range

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

1 cycling stop along this route · Cariboo Chilcotin Coast

158 km · Weekend or moreEpicComfortable for mostAlmost all gravelLots of singletrackRough, near-MTBMax climbingRemote stretches

If variety is the spice of life, this is a five-star route: dreamy doubletrack, rowdy descents, open alpine freeriding along Nine Mile Ridge, and one grueling push to earn it. The 158 km loop near Lillooet is remote even by BC standards, with no real bail options, so pack light and be ready to fix anything. Even the fittest riders manage about 40 km a day. Marmots, bighorn sheep, and grizzlies live here.
