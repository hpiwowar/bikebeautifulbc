How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Moderate, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

An extension of the Burnaby Mountain Urban Trail, this 21 km one-way route runs from Lougheed to Gastown, trading forest for waterfront and finishing among historic brick streets. SkyTrain stations at both ends make the logistics effortless: ride one way, train back.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, kʷikʷəƛ̓əm, Qayqayt, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
