Burnaby Orbit

A ride mapped and described by Love Machine

235 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

48 km · 620 m up · Full day

AmbitiousLow-traffic ridingMostly gravelLots of climbing

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing.

A 48 km loop connecting the Trans Canada Trail on Burnaby Mountain with the gravel trails around Burnaby Lake, on mostly smooth, well-graded surfaces. Best ridden clockwise, with a detour option to Port Moody's breweries and cafes.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, kʷikʷəƛ̓əm, Qayqayt, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
