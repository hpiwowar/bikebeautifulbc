Campbell River

A ride mapped and described by Elbow Valley Cycle Club

82 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

33 km · Full dayEasyRide with trafficMostly pavedGentle grades

An easy 33 km rolling ride around Campbell River, taking in the oceanfront and the town's edges with barely 150 m of climbing. Campbell River calls itself the salmon capital of the world, and the seawalk's fishing pier makes a fitting stop.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Elbow Valley Cycle Club.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
