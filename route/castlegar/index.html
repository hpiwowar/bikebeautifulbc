BT700 Castlegar

A ride mapped and described by BT 700 Bikepacking

120 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

304 km · ExpeditionAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelChunky gravelSome climbingRemote stretches

A 304 km multi-day BT700 stretch running down to Castlegar, about three-quarters unpaved with 3,500 m of climbing spread over gentle grades. The surfaces are the challenge here rather than the hills: expect long rough stretches where fatter tires and patience both pay off.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on BT 700 Bikepacking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
