Central Valley Greenway

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

433 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

25 km · AfternoonModerateComfortable for mostMostly pavedSome climbing

The Central Valley Greenway runs 25 km from Vancouver through Burnaby to New Westminster, flat, mostly paved, and stitched with parkland and riverside stretches. SkyTrain stations along the corridor make it easy to ride exactly as far as you feel like and train home.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
