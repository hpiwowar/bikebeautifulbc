Cherry Blossom Populaire

A ride mapped and described by British Columbia Randonneurs

727 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

102 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly pavedGentle grades

A 102 km populaire from Vancouver in the BC Randonneurs tradition: a friendly, non-competitive distance ride timed for spring, when the city's cherry trees are in bloom. Nearly flat and nearly all paved, it is a fine first step into randonneuring or simply a good long day out. Ride the route any time; the club event happens once a year.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on British Columbia Randonneurs.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
