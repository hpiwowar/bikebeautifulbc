Chute Lake Charcuterie Route, Kelowna

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

66 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

103 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelRough, near-MTBLots of climbing

Kelowna's introduction to bikepacking: climb out of the city on the Mission Creek Greenway and Myra-Bellevue's singletrack to the Kettle Valley Rail Trail, spend the night at Chute Lake, in the lodge's glamping tents or the free rec site next door, then descend flowy trails back to City Park. The KVR stretch runs sandy and potholed, so a mountain bike is happiest. Beaches, restaurants, and breweries wait downtown when you roll back in.
