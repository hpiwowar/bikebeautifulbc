Cross Canada Far North (BC Only)

A ride described at Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

How we describe routes →

The British Columbia portion of the Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society's Far North route from Prince Rupert to Calgary: about 1,030 km of paved touring across the northern interior, through the Cariboo and the Thompson Okanagan, before crossing into Alberta. Around 9,000 m of climbing in all, with long remote stretches and highway riding throughout. A serious self-supported tour through country most cyclists never see.

Museum of Northern British Columbia: North Coast museum of Tsimshian history and art, in a monumental longhouse building. · Prince Rupert

Ancient Forest / Chun T'oh Whudujut Provincial Park: Thousand-year-old cedars in the world's only inland temperate rainforest; boardwalk off Highway 16. · Bend

Cafe Mount Robson: operating for over 100 years at Mount Robson Provincial Park...gateway to the tallest peak in the Canadian Rockies and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. · Mount Robson

Moose Lake: Tranquil mountain lake beside the Yellowhead in the Mount Robson corridor. · Red Pass

Mount Robson Store & Gift Shop: Operating for over 100 years... at the foot of Mount Robson Provincial Park, the highest peak in the Canadian Rockies and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. · Mount Robson

🪶Traditional territories

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the As’in’i’wa’chi Niy’yaw Askiy (Rocky Mountain Cree), Dakeł Keyoh (ᑕᗸᒡ ᗲᘏᑋ), Dënéndeh, Gitxaała, Gitxsan Laxyip, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Kitselas, Kitsumkalum, Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Lax Kw’Alaams, La̱xyuubm Ts’msyen (Tsimshian), Lheidli T’enneh, Michif Piyii (Métis), Mountain Métis, Nadleh Whut’en, Nadut’en, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), Stellat’en, Takla, Tsuut’ina, Wet’suwet’en Yintikh, and ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐤ ᐊᐢᑭᕀ Nêhiyaw-Askiy (Plains Cree).
