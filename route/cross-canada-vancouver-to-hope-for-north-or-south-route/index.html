Cross Canada - Vancouver to Hope

A ride mapped and described by Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

1,221 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

176 km · Weekend or moreModerateRide with trafficMostly pavedSome climbing

Every cross-Canada tour has a first day, and this is the Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society's: 176 km from Vancouver to Hope through the Fraser Valley, nearly flat until the mountains gather at the valley's end. About 1,270 m of climbing, almost all paved, and mostly on roads you will share with traffic. From Hope, the northern and southern cross-country routes part ways.
