How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of some loose surface + some climbing.

A 73 km route near Abbotsford following the Vedder River past local food stops, then climbing forest service roads before descending to Cultus Lake. An optional shortcut trims it to about 50 km.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), and Stz’uminus.
