Discomfort Zone (Thrill Seeker Version)

A ride mapped and described by Love Machine

420 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

60 km · Full dayAmbitiousLow-traffic ridingMixed surfaceLots of climbing

A 60 km loop from Chinatown in Vancouver to the North Shore, taking on classic North Shore mountain bike descents (Ned's and Bobsled) before mellowing into smoother gravel back to the Second Narrows. Minimum 48 mm tires recommended.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Qayqayt, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
