Duncan to Chemainus

A ride mapped and described by Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

129 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

108 km · 729 m up · Weekend or more

AmbitiousShares roads with trafficMostly pavedSome climbing

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum, plus some climbing.

A rolling 108 km paved day ride between Duncan, the City of Totems, and Chemainus, famous for its outdoor murals, through the Cowichan Valley's farmland. About 730 m of climbing on shared roads; the two towns' public art makes better-than-average turnaround entertainment.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

Duncan is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. Buses and ferries will carry you and your bike there:

North Cowichan (Chemainus & Crofton) (best: bus) · Victoria (best: bus) · Nanaimo (best: bus) · Vancouver (best: ferry + bus)
