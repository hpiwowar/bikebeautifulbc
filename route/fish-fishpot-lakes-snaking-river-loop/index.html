Fish, Fishpot Lakes & Snaking River Loop

A ride mapped and described by The Great Community Route Celebration

30 cycling stops along this route · Cariboo Chilcotin Coast

346 km · 4,005 m up · Expedition

ChallengingSome traffic sharingMostly gravelSome climbingRemote stretches

Difficulty: Challenging, because of lots of climbing + long stretches far from services.

A 346 km loop starting and ending in downtown Quesnel, wandering Cariboo backroads past Fish Lake, the Fishpot Lakes, and the Snaking River. About 70% gravel, with roughly 4,000 m of climbing spread over several days. Very rustic and far between services, so sort out your resupply before leaving town.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Dakeł Keyoh (ᑕᗸᒡ ᗲᘏᑋ), Dënéndeh, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), and Tŝilhqot’in Nen.
