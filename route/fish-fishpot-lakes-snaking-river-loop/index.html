Fish, Fishpot Lakes & Snaking River Loop

A ride described at The Great Community Route Celebration

How we describe routes →

A 346 km loop starting and ending in downtown Quesnel, wandering Cariboo backroads past Fish Lake, the Fishpot Lakes, and the Snaking River. About 70% gravel, with roughly 4,000 m of climbing spread over several days. Very rustic and far between services, so sort out your resupply before leaving town.
