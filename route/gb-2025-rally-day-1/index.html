Rally Day 1 (West Vancouver)

A ride described at Gravel Buddies

How we describe routes →

The first day of the Gravel Buddies 2025 Rally: 160 km from West Vancouver, ferry across the strait included, onto Vancouver Island's mixed gravel and pavement with about 1,200 m of rolling climbing. A long social day built for steady groups; a gentler alternative route runs the same day.
