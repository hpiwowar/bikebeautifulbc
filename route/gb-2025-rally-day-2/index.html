How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum, plus some climbing.

Day two of the Gravel Buddies 2025 Rally crosses the Cowichan Valley: 118 km, more than half gravel, with about 800 m of rolling climbing through farm and wine country. A shorter alternative runs the same day for anyone saving their legs.
