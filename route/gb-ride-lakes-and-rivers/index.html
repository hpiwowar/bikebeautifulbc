Lakes and Rivers

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

368 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

50 km · Full dayModerateComfortable for mostMixed surfaceSome climbing

A Gravel Buddies favourite: 50 km wandering through parks and along rivers east of Vancouver, nearly flat, with plenty of spots to stop, swim a toe, and picnic. Perfect for a sunny day when the ride is the destination.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Kwantlen, kʷikʷəƛ̓əm, Qayqayt, Quw’utsun, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), sc̓əwaθenaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsawwassen), Semiahmoo, səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), sq̓əc̓iy̓aɁɬ təməxʷ (Katzie), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
