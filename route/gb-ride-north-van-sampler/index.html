North Van Sampler

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

196 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

56 km · Full dayAmbitiousComfortable for mostMixed surfaceLots of climbing

A 56 km sampler of North Vancouver's mixed terrain, stitching gravel paths and quiet streets with over 1,000 m of climbing. The North Shore tilts: expect to work on the way up and grin on the way down.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Gravel Buddies.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
