Cowichan Valley: Stoltz Pool to Nanaimo

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

184 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

78 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMixed surfaceGentle grades

The second leg of a Cowichan Valley crossing: 78 km from the swimming holes at Stoltz Pool on the Cowichan River north to Nanaimo, mostly paved with gravel stretches and about 400 m of climbing. Pairs with the Swartz Bay leg for a car-free island traverse ending at Nanaimo's ferries.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Gravel Buddies.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
