Golden Ears Overnighter (out and back)

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

138 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

105 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelSome climbing

An adventure that starts from your doorstep: ride 105 km out and back from Port Moody to Golden Ears, camp beneath the mountains, and be home for dinner the next day. Mostly flat on a mix of gravel and pavement, it is one of the easiest ways in the Lower Mainland to try a first bikepacking trip without a car shuttle.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Gravel Buddies.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
