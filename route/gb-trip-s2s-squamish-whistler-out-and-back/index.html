Squamish-Whistler (out and back)

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

17 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

96 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbing

The Squamish-to-Whistler stretch of the Sea to Sky Trail, ridden as a 96 km out-and-back so no shuttle is needed. The trail follows a corridor Coast Salish and Interior Salish peoples travelled long before the highway, climbing from lush rainforest into pine forest on a mix of flowy singletrack and chunkier wide sections, with clear lakes for swimming and viewpoints all along. About 1,600 m of climbing spread over the day or a night out.
