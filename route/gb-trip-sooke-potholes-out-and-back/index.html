Sooke Potholes (out and back)

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

210 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

158 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMostly gravelGentle grades

The beloved beginner overnighter: from Swartz Bay, follow the Lochside Trail through Saanich farmland and the Galloping Goose's rail-trail wilderness to Sooke Potholes Provincial Park, about 75 km each way with barely a hill, 95% of it on trail. Camp near the river and spend the evening swimming in the famous rock pools; the park squeezes cyclists in even when sites run out. Bike shops and resupply sit right on the route, and the trail never strays far from help. Bring cash for the campground and a book for the beach.
