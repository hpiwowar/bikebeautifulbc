Sunshine Coast Overnighter

A ride mapped and described by Gravel Buddies

61 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

67 km · Full dayAmbitiousRide with trafficMixed surfaceLots of climbing

A 67 km overnight trip on the lower Sunshine Coast starting near the Langdale ferry, mostly on paved roads that roll and climb hard, about 1,100 m in total. Ferry over from Horseshoe Bay, camp partway, and let the coast set the pace. Expect to share the road on the busier stretches.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Gravel Buddies.
