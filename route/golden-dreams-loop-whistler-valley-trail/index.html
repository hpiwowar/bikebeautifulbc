How we describe routes →

The Whistler Valley Trail links the resort's lakes on paved, nearly flat path: the classic 15 km loop passes Lost Lake, Green Lake, and Alta Lake, and the 26 km five-lake version adds Nita and Alpha via Creekside. Mountain views the whole way, swimming docks in summer, and family-friendly from end to end.
