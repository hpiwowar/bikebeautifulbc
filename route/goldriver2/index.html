Gold River 2

A ride mapped and described by Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

6 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

109 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousRide with trafficMostly pavedLots of climbingRemote stretches

An out-and-back paved ride on Highway 28 from the Elk Portal parking area to Gold River and on to the salt water at Muchalat Inlet, about 109 km with 1,400 m of climbing. Quiet mountain highway riding through the edge of Strathcona country, with long empty stretches, so carry what you need.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
