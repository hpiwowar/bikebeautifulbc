How we describe routes →

The Harbour Loop circles Burrard Inlet for 32 km, linking Vancouver and North Vancouver's waterfronts by bridge, with container cranes, shipyards, seawall, and historic Gastown along the way. Flat and paved, its length is the only challenge, and the SeaBus offers a shortcut if the day runs long.

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Qayqayt, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
