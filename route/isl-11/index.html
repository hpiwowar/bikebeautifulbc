How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Moderate, because of some climbing.

The Salish Sea Trail links the South Island's best rail trails, the Lochside, the Galloping Goose, and the Cowichan Valley Trail, into one big 175 km loop with a Salt Spring Island ferry hop in the middle. Most of it is calm rail-trail riding through forest and farmland; the road stretches through wine country roll and climb a bit more. Ride a 15 km piece, or take a few days and go all the way around.
