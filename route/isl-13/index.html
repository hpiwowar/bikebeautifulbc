Pender Islands

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

28 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

25 km · AfternoonModerateRide with trafficMostly paved

Reached by ferry, the Pender Islands offer 6 to 25 km of winding, hilly coastal roads past vineyards, parkland, and dozens of little beach accesses. Roads are narrow and shared in the Gulf Islands way; the hills are constant and the pace unhurried by law of local custom.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
