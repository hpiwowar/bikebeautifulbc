Shawnigan Lake & Cobble Hill

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

40 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

80 km · Full dayEasyRide with trafficMostly paved

Around Shawnigan Lake and Cobble Hill, loops of 12 to 80 km roll through the Cowichan's wine country: the signed 35 km Wine Route, the lake circuit past historic Shawnigan village, and connections toward Mill Bay's ferry. Vineyards, cideries, and farm stands supply the stops; the roads are shared and gently rolling.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
