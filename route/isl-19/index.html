How we describe routes →

Around Shawnigan Lake and Cobble Hill, loops of 12 to 80 km roll through the Cowichan's wine country: the signed 35 km Wine Route, the lake circuit past historic Shawnigan village, and connections toward Mill Bay's ferry. Vineyards, cideries, and farm stands supply the stops; the roads are shared and gently rolling.
