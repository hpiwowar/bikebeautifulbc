Duncan

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

20 km · Afternoon · Vancouver Island

EasySome traffic sharingMostly paved

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Around Duncan, the City of Totems, short loops of 2 to 20 km link rail trail, dyke paths, parkland, and pockets of singletrack, with the town's famous totem collection and public art never far away. Quiet, flat, and family-friendly, with good birdwatching along the river dykes.
