North Cowichan

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

97 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

27 km · AfternoonEasyRide with trafficMostly paved

North Cowichan's loops run 3 to 27 km through farmland and along the sea, taking in Maple Bay, Genoa Bay's floating village, Quamichan Lake, and historic Cowichan Bay with its heritage waterfront and bakeries. Breweries and a ferry-hop option round it out; some hills and shared roads keep it honest.
