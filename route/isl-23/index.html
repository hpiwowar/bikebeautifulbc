Nanaimo South

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

143 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

32 km · Full dayEasyRide with trafficMostly paved

South of Nanaimo, loops of 6 to 32 km explore the old coal country: the Morden Colliery rail trail and its historic tipple, Nanaimo River parkland, Hemer Park's lakeside trails, and the Yellow Point farm lanes. Quiet riding with history underfoot, and some shared-road stretches between segments.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
