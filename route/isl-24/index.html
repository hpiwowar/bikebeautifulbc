Nanaimo

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

197 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

30 km · Full dayEasyComfortable for mostMostly paved

Nanaimo's rides run 3 to 30 km through town: the harbourfront walkway, the E&N rail trail, Westwood Lake's swimming loop, and best of all a foot-ferry hop to Saysutshun (Newcastle Island), a car-free provincial park with 9 km of shoreline trail. Flat, family-friendly, and transit-connected throughout.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.
