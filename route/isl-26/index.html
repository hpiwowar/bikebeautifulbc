How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Moderate, because of some climbing.

North of Nanaimo, flat and family-friendly rides of 3.5 to 60 km follow the Parkway Trail and E&N rail trail up through seaside parkland to Lantzville. Transit access along the corridor makes one-way rides easy, and singletrack parks sit just off the line for anyone wanting more.
