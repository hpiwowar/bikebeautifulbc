Nanaimo North

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

176 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

60 km · Full dayModerateCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

North of Nanaimo, flat and family-friendly rides of 3.5 to 60 km follow the Parkway Trail and E&N rail trail up through seaside parkland to Lantzville. Transit access along the corridor makes one-way rides easy, and singletrack parks sit just off the line for anyone wanting more.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
