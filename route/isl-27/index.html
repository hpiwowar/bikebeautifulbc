Nanoose Bay - Parksville

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

55 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

40 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMostly paved

Between Nanoose Bay and Parksville, loops of 3.5 to 40 km wander forest, farmland, and the peninsula's seaside viewpoints, from the Fairwinds loop to the Errington back roads. Hillier than the coastal flats nearby, with singletrack options and quiet lanes, though highway-adjacent sections carry traffic.
