Rathtrevor Park - Top Bridge Trail

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

46 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

30 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMixed surface

Loops of 5 to 30 km link Rathtrevor Beach's forest paths, the Top Bridge Trail with its river-canyon suspension bridge and swimming rocks, and the Englishman River parkland into Parksville. Family-friendly at the beach end, more singletrack-flavoured along the river, with some shared-road connections.
