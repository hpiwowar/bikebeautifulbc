How we describe routes →

Between Qualicum Bay and Royston, the old Oceanside highway serves up flat, view-rich seaside touring of 9 to 77 km, with Fanny Bay's oysters en route and a ferry hop to Denman Island's quiet lanes as the detour of choice. Shared roads throughout, but the shoulder is generous and the sea does the entertaining.

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, ɬaʔamɩn gɩǰɛ (Tla’amin), Te’mexw Treaty Association, We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
