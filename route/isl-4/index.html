How we describe routes →

Victoria's city loops run 1.6 to 20 km through the capital's best: the Inner Harbour and Ogden Point breakwater, Beacon Hill Park and its roaming peacocks, Craigdarroch Castle's neighbourhood, and Canada's oldest Chinatown. Flat, family-friendly, transit-connected, and dense with museums, gardens, and public art.

Royal BC Museum: BC's provincial museum on the Inner Harbour; natural history and First Peoples galleries. · Victoria

British Columbia Parliament Buildings: BC's 1898 Parliament Buildings on the Inner Harbour; free public tours. · Victoria

The Fairmont Empress Hotel: landmark Edwardian chateau-style hotel... since 1908... one of the most historically significant grand hotels in Canada. · Victoria

Big Bad John's: BC's first-ever licensed cocktail lounge, opened in 1954. · Victoria

Craigdarroch Castle: Coal baron Dunsmuir's 1890 castle, a National Historic Site above Victoria. · Victoria
