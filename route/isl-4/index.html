Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Victoria's city loops run 1.6 to 20 km through the capital's best: the Inner Harbour and Ogden Point breakwater, Beacon Hill Park and its roaming peacocks, Craigdarroch Castle's neighbourhood, and Canada's oldest Chinatown. Flat, family-friendly, transit-connected, and dense with museums, gardens, and public art.
