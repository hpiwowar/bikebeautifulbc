Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

The Comox Valley Circle loops 65 km through the valley's farm and cidery country, linking seaside and riverside stretches with rail-trail and parkland. Essentially flat, but its length and shared-road sections make it a full day. Farm stands and tasting rooms supply the excuses to stop.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, ɬaʔamɩn gɩǰɛ (Tla’amin), We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
