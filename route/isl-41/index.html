East Courtenay - Comox

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

137 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

36 km · Full day

EasySome traffic sharingMostly paved

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Loops of 5 to 36 km connect East Courtenay and Comox, from the Three Beaches loop along the shoreline to the Seal Bay forest trails and the full 36 km Red Loop. Family-friendly, nearly all paved, with waterfront parks, harbour views, and frequent wildlife.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, ɬaʔamɩn gɩǰɛ (Tla’amin), We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
