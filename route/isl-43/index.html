Frog Greenways Loop

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

62 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

28 km · AfternoonEasyCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

Campbell River's signed FROG Greenways Loop makes a flat, family-friendly 28 km circuit of the city's waterfront, riverside, and forested parkland, with good birdwatching and the Beaver Lodge Lands' easy singletrack as an optional add-on. Follow the frog signs and let the route do the navigating.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
