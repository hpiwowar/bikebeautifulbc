Quadra Island

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

17 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

30 km · Full day

AmbitiousSome traffic sharingMixed surface

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing + some loose surface.

A ten-minute ferry from Campbell River, Quadra Island offers loops of 3 to 30 km: Rebecca Spit's beach-lined provincial park, the Cape Mudge lighthouse loop through We Wai Kai territory, and quiet forested roads between. Steeper than it looks in places, with a winery stop and singletrack networks for the curious.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Homalco, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, K’ómoks, Liǧʷiłdax̌ʷ, ɬaʔamɩn gɩǰɛ (Tla’amin), ƛohos giǰi (Klahoose), We Wai Kai, and Wei Wai Kum.
