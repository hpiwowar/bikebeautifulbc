Seaside Loop

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

40 km · Full day · Vancouver Island

EasyLow-traffic ridingMixed surface

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Victoria's signed Seaside Touring Route follows the shoreline for up to 40 km, from the breakwater at Ogden Point around clifftop viewpoints and beaches toward Cordova Bay, with shorter neighbourhood loops of 2 km and up branching off. Urban riding with constant ocean views, some honest hills, and historic stops throughout.
