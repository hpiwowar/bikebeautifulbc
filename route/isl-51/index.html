North Sunshine Coast

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

34 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

180 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousComfortable for mostMixed surface

The north Sunshine Coast offers rides from a 5.5 km Okeover Arm amble to the coast's big adventures: the ferry-to-ferry road run, the 13 km Inland Lake loop on one of BC's most accessible trails, quiet roads to Lund at the end of Highway 101, and for the ambitious, trail options along the 180 km Sunshine Coast Trail corridor. Forest, lakes, and waterfront throughout, with real hills on the longer routes.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
