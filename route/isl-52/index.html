Texada Island • Savary Island

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

4 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

100 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelChunky gravel

A ferry-hop pairing with two very different personalities: Texada, the big quiet island of gravel roads, forest, and honest hills, ridden in sections from 16 to 48 km, and little Savary, 18 km of sandy lanes rimmed by some of BC's warmest beaches, reached by water taxi. Together they make 18 to 100 km of seaside riding with wildlife, history, and hardly any traffic.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.
