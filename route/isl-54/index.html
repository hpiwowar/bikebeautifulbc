Egmont to Secret Cove

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

21 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

36 km · Full dayChallengingCalm streets, some sharingMixed surfaceChunky gravel

This point-to-point route explores the upper Sunshine Coast between Egmont and Secret Cove, with variations from 3 to 36 km taking in Garden Bay, Francis Point, and the Suncoaster Trail. The country is hilly and forested, the ocean never far, and the side trip to Skookumchuck Narrows, where the tide boils through at up to 30 cm a second, is worth planning around the current tables. Expect real climbing and some rough surfaces.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.
