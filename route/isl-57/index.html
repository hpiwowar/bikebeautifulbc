Gibsons

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

105 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

55 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMostly paved

A ferry ride from Horseshoe Bay lands you in Gibsons, where rides run from a 4 km esplanade amble to a 55 km day taking in the Ocean Beach Esplanade, quiet forested roads toward Port Mellon, and connections down the coast to Roberts Creek. The town's brewery and harbour cafes anchor the start and finish. The hills are real on the longer options, and the highway stretches carry traffic.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, shíshálh swíya (Sechelt), and Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish).
