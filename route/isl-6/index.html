Glen Lake / Royal Roads / Metchosin

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

24 km · Afternoon · Vancouver Island

AmbitiousSome traffic sharingMixed surface

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing.

Loops around Glen Lake, Royal Roads, and Metchosin span 4.5 to 24 km west of Victoria, passing Hatley Castle's gardens at Royal Roads, lakeside swimming at Glen Lake, and Metchosin's rural seaside lanes. Family-friendly overall, though the Metchosin hills roll more than the lake sections.
