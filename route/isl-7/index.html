E&N Loops / Esquimalt / View Royal

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

432 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

46 km · Full dayModerateComfortable for mostMixed surface

Around Esquimalt and View Royal, flat, family-friendly loops of 2 to 46 km combine the E&N Rail Trail and Galloping Goose with waterfront rides along the Gorge, Portage Inlet's bird flats, and Thetis Lake's swimming beaches. Mix and match trail sections to size the day; the 19 km E&N-plus-Goose loop is the classic.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
