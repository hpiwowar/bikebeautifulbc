How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

The Saanich Centennial Trails knit the municipality's greenways into rides of 1 to 45 km: the Colquitz River's salmon stream, Elk Lake's loop, the old Interurban rail grade, and connecting corridors between parks. Flat, quiet, family-friendly riding with good birdwatching, all inside the capital region.
