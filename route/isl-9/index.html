North Saanich

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

164 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

60 km · Full dayEasyCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

North Saanich offers rides of 3 to 60 km around the peninsula's tip: the Flight Path loop circling the airport, Lands End's quiet lanes, Sidney's seaside village, and practical ferry-to-ferry connections for travellers. Farmland, sea views, and gentle hills, family-friendly and transit-linked throughout.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
