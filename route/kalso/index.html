An 85 km BT700 leg with one of BC's more memorable trail moments: a hand-pulled cable-car river crossing on the Galena Trail out of New Denver. From there the Wagon Trail runs rough and chunky into Kaslo, then paved highway follows Kootenay Lake south to Balfour, with about 2,100 m of climbing along the way. Route created by Matt Kadey of BT 700 Bikepacking.

☕Stops worth stopping for36 along this route

S.S. Moyie National Historic Site: World's oldest intact passenger sternwheeler (1898), a National Historic Site on Kaslo's waterfront. · Kaslo

Ainsworth Hot Springs Resort: natural mineral hot spring pools including a remarkable 150-foot horseshoe cave ... used for healing by the Ktunaxa people since time immemorial. · Ainsworth Hot Springs

Japanese Canadian Museum: Museum of the WWII Japanese Canadian internment, in the Kaslo building where internees lived. · Kaslo

The Langham: Provincial Heritage Site in Kaslo: galleries, a 70-seat theatre, and the internment museum. · Kaslo

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
