Kettle Valley Railway Midway Loop

How we describe routes →

A 352 km loop from Midway through Myra Canyon's Kettle Valley rail grade, then wine country roads around Naramata, Penticton, Oliver, and Osoyoos, back over Anarchist Summit. Mapped by Penny Lawless, it's rated easy to moderate.

☕Stops worth stopping for228 along this route

Kettle Valley Rail Trail: Myra Canyon's 18 trestles and two tunnels on the Kettle Valley Rail Trail, a National Historic Site. · Myra

SS Sicamous: Restored 1914 sternwheeler SS Sicamous, moored on Penticton's Okanagan Lake shore. · Penticton

Daydreamer Wines: Marcus holds the Master of Wine designation; Best Red Wine in Canada in 2019; multiple platinum and gold medals at the National Wine Awards of Canada. · Naramata

Kettle River Museum: Mile 0 of the Kettle Valley Railway: the original 1901 station house at Midway. · Midway

Lang Vineyards: British Columbia's first farm-gate winery … home to the oldest vines on the Naramata Bench. · Naramata

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), sx̌ʷyʔiɬp (Colville), and Syilx (Okanagan).
