How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of chunky surface + some climbing.

A 352 km loop from Midway through Myra Canyon's Kettle Valley rail grade, then wine country roads around Naramata, Penticton, Oliver, and Osoyoos, back over Anarchist Summit. Mapped by Penny Lawless, it's rated easy to moderate.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), sx̌ʷyʔiɬp (Colville), and Syilx (Okanagan).
