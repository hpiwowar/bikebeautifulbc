How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing + some loose surface.

A roughly 1,200 km gravel-focused loop from Fernie through the Flathead into Montana, Idaho, and Washington and back into the Kootenays, following portions of the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route and BC Epic 1000. Mapped by Megan Dunn, it mixes forest service roads, some pavement, a short singletrack stretch, and one demanding hike-a-bike descent through bear country, with a free ferry crossing of Kootenay Lake near Nelson.

Traditional territories#

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Kalispel, Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
