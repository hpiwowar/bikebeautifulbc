Lac du Bois Tranquility, Kamloops

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

29 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

229 km · Weekend or moreChallengingComfortable for mostMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

North of Kamloops lies a maze of scenic gravel through grasslands, mossy forest, and deep canyons that could be straight out of a Western. This 229 km loop through the Lac du Bois Grasslands climbs to Vidette Lake and back over three to four days, past forgotten homesteads, the Castle Rock Hoodoos, and country thick with stories, from Sasquatch sightings at Red Lake to gold panning on the Tranquille River. Long steep climbs, exposed heat, and no resupply, so carry water and take your time. Lakeside rec sites make camping easy.
