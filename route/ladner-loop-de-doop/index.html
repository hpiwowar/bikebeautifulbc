Ladner Loop-de-Doop

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

118 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

34 km · Full dayEasyCalm streets, some sharingMixed surfaceGentle grades

The Ladner Loop-de-Doop covers 38 km of Delta's riverside, seaside, and farmland, dead flat despite the distance, with historic Ladner Village for coffee and the bay's birdlife for company. A long easy ride rather than a hard one.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
