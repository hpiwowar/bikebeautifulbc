Lakes and Hot Springs Loop

How we describe routes →

A 550 km figure-eight route through the Central Kootenays from Nakusp, mapped by Ali Becker and Mat Leblanc, passing fourteen lakes, three hot springs, and two free ferry crossings plus a cable car over Carpenter Creek. Moderate to challenging, mostly for its remoteness through grizzly, black bear, cougar, and wolf territory.

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
