Loop Kaslo Slocan

A ride mapped and described by Trails BC

109 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

221 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousRide with trafficMixed surfaceSome climbing

A 221 km loop through the West Kootenay linking Nelson, Kaslo, and New Denver over two days, with an optional cable-car river crossing on the way out and a return leg on the Slocan Valley Rail Trail. About 2,300 m of climbing in all, with a fair amount of highway riding between the trail sections, so expect to share the road.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Trails BC.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
