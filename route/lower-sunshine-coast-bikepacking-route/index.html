Lower Sunshine Coast Bikepacking Route

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

48 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

101 km · Weekend or moreEpicComfortable for mostAlmost all gravelLots of singletrackChunky gravelMax climbing

A forty-minute ferry from Vancouver drops you at Earls Cove, where this route dives into a green tunnel of loamy mountain bike trails made famous by the BC Bike Race, wooden stunts and all. Three-quarters of the 101 km is singletrack, with around 3,100 m of climbing, so this is proper technical mountain biking, not a gravel ride. Sechelt sits at halfway for resupply, coffee, and brunch, and swims at Chapman Creek and Crowston Lake break up the effort. A brilliant first bikepacking trip for confident mountain bikers.
