How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

A 159 km populaire starting in Langley, in the BC Randonneurs tradition of long, sociable, non-competitive rides. Rolling Fraser Valley terrain with about 800 m of climbing, mostly paved with a few gravel stretches, and enough distance to make the finish feel earned. Ride it any time; the club event runs once a year. Expect to share the road.
