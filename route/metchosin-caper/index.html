How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum, plus some climbing.

An 83 km rolling loop out of Victoria through Metchosin's rural roads and trails, about a third unpaved with 800 m of climbing. Farm stands, ocean glimpses, and forest stretches trade places the whole way around; a local's tour of the capital's quiet west.
