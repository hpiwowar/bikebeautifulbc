Mill Bay Rambler

A ride described at Bike Gear Database

100 km · 1,136 m up · Weekend or more · Vancouver Island

AmbitiousSome traffic sharingMostly gravelSome climbing

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum, plus some climbing.

A 100 km road ride from Esquimalt up the Saanich Inlet coast toward Mill Bay and back, with about 1,100 m of climbing. Classic South Island road touring: shoreline stretches, forested climbs, and enough distance to make the coffee stop count.
