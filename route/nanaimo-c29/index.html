Nanaimo Cycling Trail Loop

A ride mapped and described by Trails BC

216 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

63 km · Full dayEasyComfortable for mostMixed surfaceGentle grades

A 63 km loop through Nanaimo's greenways and parks, linking Bowen Park's forest, Petroglyph Provincial Park's ancient rock carvings, the harbourfront walkway, and the city's rail trails, with a ferry side trip to car-free Saysutshun (Newcastle Island) the highlight worth building in. Mostly flat; allow four to six hours with stops.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Trails BC.
