North Shore Spirit Trail

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

245 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

12 km · 16 m up · Afternoon

EasyLow-traffic ridingMostly pavedGentle grades

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

The North Shore Spirit Trail follows the waterfront for 15 km one way between North Vancouver and West Vancouver, flat, paved, and strung with public art, shipyard history, and harbour views. SeaBus access at Lonsdale makes it an easy car-free outing, and the market at the Shipyards anchors the middle.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

North Vancouver is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. Buses, the SeaBus, ferries, and the SkyTrain will carry you and your bike there:

West Vancouver (best: bus) · Vancouver (best: SeaBus) · Nanaimo (best: ferry + bus) · Victoria (best: bus + SkyTrain + SeaBus)

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
