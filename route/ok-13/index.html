Fur Brigade Trail

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

64 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

43 km · Full dayModerateCalm streets, some sharingMixed surface

An out-and-back on the historic Fur Brigade Trail, the old Hudson's Bay Company route between Summerland and Peachland. Ride 4 km or the full 43, climbing quietly through forest to big views, with wildlife common along the way. Partly gravel, and steeper than the average rail trail: this was a pack trail, not a train line.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
