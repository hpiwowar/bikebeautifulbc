West Kelowna

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

21 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

40 km · Full dayAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

West Kelowna's out-and-back covers 3 to 40 km, linking lakefront parks with the vineyards, farmland, and breweries of the west side. Family-friendly near the water, hillier as you head for the wineries.
