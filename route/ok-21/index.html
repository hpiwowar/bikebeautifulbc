How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Moderate, because of some climbing.

North of Kelowna, the Okanagan Rail Trail continues through Lake Country toward Vernon, 8 to 75 km out and back, famously hugging the turquoise shoreline of Kalamalka Lake. Vineyards, farmland, and lakefront the whole way, gorgeous in fall colour, and gentle enough for families.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

Vernon is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. Buses, ferries, and the SkyTrain will carry you and your bike there:

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), and Syilx (Okanagan).
