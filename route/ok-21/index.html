Okanagan Rail Trail - North

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

78 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

75 km · Full dayModerateCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

North of Kelowna, the Okanagan Rail Trail continues through Lake Country toward Vernon, 8 to 75 km out and back, famously hugging the turquoise shoreline of Kalamalka Lake. Vineyards, farmland, and lakefront the whole way, gorgeous in fall colour, and gentle enough for families.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
