Okanagan Lake Views

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

58 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

30 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMostly paved

This Vernon loop runs 2.5 to 30 km, collecting Okanagan Lake views from waterfront parks, farmland, and climbing hillside roads, with historic stops along the way. The short version suits families; the full loop earns its viewpoints.

The route lives with its publisher
