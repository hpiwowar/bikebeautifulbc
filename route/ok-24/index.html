Vernon Exploring

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

98 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

13 km · AfternoonEasyCalm streets, some sharingMostly paved

Vernon Exploring loops through Vernon and Coldstream for 8 to 13 km, stringing together urban parks, the city's famous murals and public art, historic sites, and breweries. A few hills, but nothing a coffee stop can't fix.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
