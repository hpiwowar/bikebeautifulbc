Swan Lake & Grey Canal

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

77 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

27 km · AfternoonChallengingComfortable for mostMostly paved

Swan Lake and the historic Grey Canal near Vernon form a loop of 1.3 to 27 km. The lake is a birdwatching favourite, and the canal, once the Okanagan's most ambitious irrigation project, now carries a trail through farmland and forest with wide valley views. The full loop climbs more than you might expect.
