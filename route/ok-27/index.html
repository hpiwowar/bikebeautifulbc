How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

A loop through the green farm country of Salmon Arm, Spallumcheen, and Enderby, ridden at anywhere from 24 to 74 km. Quiet farmland roads, riverside stretches, and a section of rail trail lead past vineyards and historic sites, with some climbing but nothing sustained.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), and Syilx (Okanagan).
