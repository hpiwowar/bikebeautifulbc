Notch Hill - Tappen

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

11 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

50 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMixed surface

This Shuswap loop links Tappen, Notch Hill, Sorrento, Blind Bay, and Sunnybrae over 18.5 to 50 km of quiet farmland roads. Vineyards, historic sites, and Shuswap Lake views roll past, with wildlife along the way and moderate climbing throughout.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
