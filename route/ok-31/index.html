How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of some loose surface + some climbing.

The North Shuswap out-and-back runs through Squilax, Scotch Creek, and Celista for anywhere from 5 to 84 km, past lake views, forest singletrack, and vineyards. This is salmon country: Tsútswecw Provincial Park hosts one of North America's great sockeye runs each fall, spectacular in its dominant years. Some climbing on the longer options.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

The transit network doesn’t quite reach this ride. The closest doorway is Sicamous, about 18 km from the route. Buses, ferries, and the SkyTrain will carry you and your bike that far:
