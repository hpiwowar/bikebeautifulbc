Kettle Valley Rail Trail - Hope to Summerland

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

How we describe routes →

The western half of the Kettle Valley Rail Trail links Hope to Summerland through Brookmere, Tulameen, Princeton, and Osprey Lake, with access points letting you ride anything from 26 km to the full 254. Quiet, historic rail grade through forest and riverside country, finishing in wine country. Be aware that parts of the corridor near the Coquihalla are currently closed or rough, so check current trail status before planning a through-ride.

Hope is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. Buses, the SkyTrain, and ferries will carry you and your bike there:

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), Stz’uminus, and Syilx (Okanagan).
