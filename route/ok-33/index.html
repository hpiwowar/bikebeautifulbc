Columbia & Western Rail Trail

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

69 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

162 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMixed surfaceChunky gravelRemote stretches

The Columbia & Western Rail Trail crosses the Boundary and West Kootenay regions from Midway through Greenwood, Grand Forks, and Christina Lake up to Farron and down to Castlegar, with access points along its 21 to 162 km. The historic corridor is quiet and rich with wildlife and birdwatching, but do not expect groomed rail trail the whole way: long stretches run rough and chunky, shared with ATVs, so bigger tires and a sense of adventure help.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
