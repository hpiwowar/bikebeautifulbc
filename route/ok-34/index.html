How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of a lot of riding on busier roads.

The West Kootenays kept their railway history rideable: four rail trails around Nakusp, New Denver, the Slocan Valley, Nelson, and Salmo. Ride one as a gentle riverside day, or string them together into something closer to 170 km. The trails themselves are calm and forested, and wildlife sightings are common; getting between them means some riding on shared roads.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
