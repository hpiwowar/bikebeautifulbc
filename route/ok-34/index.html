Slocan Valley - Nelson-Salmo - Nakusp

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

39 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

170 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousRide with trafficMixed surfaceRemote stretches

The West Kootenays kept their railway history rideable: four rail trails around Nakusp, New Denver, the Slocan Valley, Nelson, and Salmo. Ride one as a gentle riverside day, or string them together into something closer to 170 km. The trails themselves are calm and forested, and wildlife sightings are common; getting between them means some riding on shared roads.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
