Similkameen

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

19 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

40 km · Full dayEasyRide with trafficMostly paved

This Similkameen Valley loop through Cawston and Keremeos runs 4 to 40 km of quiet, family-friendly riding on rail trail and riverside roads. The valley calls itself Canada's organic farming capital, and the fruit stands, orchards, and vineyards along the way back the claim. Flat, warm, and generous with views.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.
