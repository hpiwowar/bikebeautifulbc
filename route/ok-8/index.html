How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Penticton Exploring loops through the city for 1.5 to 20 km of flat, family-friendly riding, linking lakefront parks, rail trail, historic sites, and the edges of wine country. The city sits between two lakes, so beaches bookend the ride.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), and Syilx (Okanagan).
