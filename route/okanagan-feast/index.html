Okanagan Feast

A ride described at Bikepack Canada

238 km · 4,598 m up · Weekend or more · Thompson Okanagan

AmbitiousLow-traffic ridingMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbing

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of lots of climbing + some loose surface.

A four-day, 238 km loop around the south end of Okanagan Lake from Kelowna, mapped by Cameron Fenton, linking rail trail, gravel backroads, and singletrack. Paced for winery, brewery, and food stops, best ridden at harvest time.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), and Syilx (Okanagan).
