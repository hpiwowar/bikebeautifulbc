ACA Pacific Coast (BC Only)

A ride mapped and described by Adventure Cycling Association

248 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

65 km · Full dayModerateRide with trafficMostly pavedSome climbing

One of Adventure Cycling's flagship routes, the Pacific Coast runs roughly 3,000 km of paved coastal touring from Vancouver to the US-Mexico border. The BC leg is its opening chapter: 65 km from Vancouver south to the Peace Arch crossing, flat to rolling on city routes and suburban highway. Modest riding on its own, but every ride to Mexico has to start somewhere.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Adventure Cycling Association.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
