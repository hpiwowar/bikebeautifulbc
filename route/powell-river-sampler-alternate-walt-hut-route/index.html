Powell River Sampler (Alternate Walt Hut Route)

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

18 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

98 km · Weekend or moreChallengingComfortable for mostAlmost all gravelChunky gravelMax climbing

An alternate version of the Powell River Sampler that builds in the climb to Walt Hill hut: a steep gravel grind and a stretch of hike-a-bike, rewarded with a cozy cabin and 360-degree views over the canoe country and across to Vancouver Island. About 98 km with 2,800 m of climbing, noticeably harder than the standard line.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on bikepacking.com.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
