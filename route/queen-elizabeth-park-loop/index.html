Queen Elizabeth Park Loop

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

731 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

20 km · AfternoonEasyComfortable for mostMostly pavedGentle grades

This 20 km loop climbs gently through Vancouver's neighbourhoods to Queen Elizabeth Park, the city's highest point, where the quarry gardens and skyline views reward the effort. Public art and quiet bikeways link the rest of the way around.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
