Santa Rosa Valleys and Vistas - Gravel Bike Friendly Version

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

64 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

202 km · Weekend or moreChallengingCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelChunky gravelMax climbing

An alternate line for the Santa Rosa Valleys and Vistas loop that keeps the Castlegar start, the rail grade, and the big Kootenay scenery while steering around the most technical singletrack. Slightly less climbing than the original, and a better match for a gravel bike than a mountain bike.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on bikepacking.com.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
