How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Challenging, because of punishing climbs + chunky surface.

A four-day loop around the Rossland Range from Castlegar, stitching the historic Columbia & Western rail grade, Bulldog Tunnel included, to gravel climbs and singletrack descents on the 1864 Dewdney Trail. History rides along the whole way, from gold-rush rail lines to the Doukhobor story told at the Discovery Centre near the finish. Plenty of climbing, guaranteed hike-a-bike, and a riverside singletrack finale above the Columbia.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
