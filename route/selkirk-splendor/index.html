A ride described at Adventure Cycling Association

How we describe routes →

The British Columbia stretch of Adventure Cycling's Selkirk Splendor, about 355 km through the Selkirk Mountains before the route drops toward Sandpoint, Idaho. Mostly paved and never flat: the BC leg piles up around 5,500 m of climbing through lake and mountain country. A committed road tour for riders happy sharing the highway.

☕Stops worth stopping for100 along this route

S.S. Moyie National Historic Site: World's oldest intact passenger sternwheeler (1898), a National Historic Site on Kaslo's waterfront. · Kaslo

Kootenay Lake Ferry: cyclists walk or roll bikes aboard as foot passengers at no charge… Unboxed bikes OK. · Nelson

Ainsworth Hot Springs Resort: natural mineral hot spring pools including a remarkable 150-foot horseshoe cave ... used for healing by the Ktunaxa people since time immemorial. · Ainsworth Hot Springs

Japanese Canadian Museum: Museum of the WWII Japanese Canadian internment, in the Kaslo building where internees lived. · Kaslo

Nelson Brewing Company: one of BC's original craft breweries, founded in 1991... first craft brewery east of the Okanagan... historic building that has housed brewing since the 1800s. · Nelson

🪶Traditional territories

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Kalispel, Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
