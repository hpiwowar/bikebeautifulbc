Selkirk Splendor (BC Only)

A ride mapped and described by Adventure Cycling Association

101 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

355 km · 5,532 m up · Expedition

ChallengingShares roads with trafficMixed surfaceLots of climbing

Difficulty: Challenging, because of a lot of riding on busier roads, plus lots of climbing.

The British Columbia stretch of Adventure Cycling's Selkirk Splendor, about 355 km through the Selkirk Mountains before the route drops toward Sandpoint, Idaho. Mostly paved and never flat: the BC leg piles up around 5,500 m of climbing through lake and mountain country. A committed road tour for riders happy sharing the highway.

Traditional territories#

The BC portion of this route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Kalispel, Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), and Syilx (Okanagan).
