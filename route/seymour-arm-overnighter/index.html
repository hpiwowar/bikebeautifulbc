How we describe routes →

A 175 km, mostly unpaved overnighter through two Monashee mountain valleys near Sicamous, mapped by Dario Dulic. Steep gravel climbs up to 20 percent grade and 3,778 m of elevation gain, with a resupply stop in the remote village of Seymour Arm.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

The transit network doesn’t quite reach this ride. The closest doorway is Sicamous, about 22 km from the route. Buses, ferries, and the SkyTrain will carry you and your bike that far:

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), and snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt).
