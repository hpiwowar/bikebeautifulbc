Squamish Cycle Loops

A ride mapped and described by Trails BC

113 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

79 km · Full dayModerateComfortable for mostMostly gravel

A set of cycling loops around Squamish, from about 47 km up to a 79 km combination, tracing Howe Sound and the Squamish, Mamquam, and Stawamus rivers on a mix of gravel paths and quiet roads. Mostly gentle grades, with mountain walls on every horizon and shorter options easy to carve out.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Trails BC.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
