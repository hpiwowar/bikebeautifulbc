Stanley Park Seawall

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

95 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

10 km · Afternoon

EasyMostly paved

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

The seawall around Stanley Park may be Canada's most famous bike ride: 10 to 12 km one-way around the park, ocean and mountains on one side, old forest and gardens on the other, with public art and viewpoints the whole way. Flat, paved, and busy; go early for the quiet version.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
