How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Moderate, because of some climbing.

A 55 km loop around Summerland taking in beaches, parks, and the high-level Lakeview Trail with views across Okanagan Lake toward Naramata, plus a stretch of the Kettle Valley Railway route past Mount Conkle and over the Trout Creek Trestle. Modest climbing and a mix of pavement and gravel make it a satisfying full-morning ride.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Nłeʔkepmx Tmíxʷ (Nlaka’pamux), and Syilx (Okanagan).
