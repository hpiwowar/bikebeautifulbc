Summerland Cycle Loop

A ride mapped and described by Trails BC

51 cycling stops along this route · Thompson Okanagan

55 km · Full dayModerateCalm streets, some sharingMixed surfaceSome climbing

A 55 km loop around Summerland taking in beaches, parks, and the high-level Lakeview Trail with views across Okanagan Lake toward Naramata, plus a stretch of the Kettle Valley Railway route past Mount Conkle and over the Trout Creek Trestle. Modest climbing and a mix of pavement and gravel make it a satisfying full-morning ride.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Trails BC.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
