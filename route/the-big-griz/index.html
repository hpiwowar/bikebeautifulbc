The Big Griz

A ride mapped and described by BT 700 Bikepacking

1,254 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

2261 km · ExpeditionEpicCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

BT700 BC flagship, 2,380 km from Victoria to Banff with about 62% on gravel and dirt and climbing that never really stops. The nine other BT700 routes here are its side trips and bypasses. Our map shows the BC leg; route files are on bt700.ca.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on BT 700 Bikepacking.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
