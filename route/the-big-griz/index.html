The Big Griz

A ride mapped and described by BT 700 Bikepacking

1,269 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

2261 km · 38,186 m up · Expedition

EpicSome traffic sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

Difficulty: Epic, because of sheer distance, plus lots of climbing.

BT700 BC flagship, 2,380 km from Victoria to Banff with about 62% on gravel and dirt and climbing that never really stops. The nine other BT700 routes here are its side trips and bypasses. Our map shows the BC leg; route files are on bt700.ca. Route created by Matt Kadey of BT 700 Bikepacking.
