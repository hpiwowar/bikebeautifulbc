The Great Granby Loop

A ride mapped and described by Bikepack Canada

75 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

477 km · 7,345 m up · Expedition

ChallengingLow-traffic ridingMostly gravelChunky gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

Difficulty: Challenging, because of chunky surface + lots of climbing.

A 475 km, five-to-seven day loop through the West Kootenays from near Castlegar, mapped by Ali Becker and Mat Leblanc. Rail trails, forest service road climbs, and paved connectors through old mining and forestry towns, with a free cable ferry and side trips to hot springs and alpine lakes.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation, Ĩyãħé Nakón mąkóce (Stoney), Ktunaxa ɁamakɁis, Secwepemcúl’ecw (Secwépemc), snʕickstx tmxʷúlaʔxʷ (Sinixt), sx̌ʷyʔiɬp (Colville), and Syilx (Okanagan).
