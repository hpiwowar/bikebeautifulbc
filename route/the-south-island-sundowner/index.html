The South Island Sundowner

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

179 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

139 km · Weekend or moreChallengingComfortable for mostMostly gravelSome climbing

A 139 km loop that never strays far from Victoria yet delivers a real taste of the island's ruggedness: rail trail and fire roads link steep, loose climbs through Thetis Lake and Francis King parks, remote singletrack in the Sooke Hills, and wide views over the Strait of Juan de Fuca to the Olympic Mountains. The numbers look tame, but a couple of kilometres of rocky hike-a-bike and relentless 20% pitches earn its reputation. Cafes and swimming lakes reward a three-day pace; strong riders chase the sundown in two.
