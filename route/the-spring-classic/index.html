The Spring Classic

A ride mapped and described by Love Machine

204 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

103 km · 159 m up · Weekend or more

EasyLow-traffic ridingMostly gravelGentle grades

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

A 103 km, mostly flat loop starting and finishing at Bridgeport Canada Line Station near Vancouver, linking seaside trails in Richmond and Iona, a stop in Steveston, and singletrack, doubletrack, and boardwalk trails in Delta. About half unpaved, transit-accessible via SkyTrain.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

Richmond is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. Buses, the SkyTrain, and ferries will carry you and your bike there:

Delta (best: bus) · Vancouver (best: SkyTrain) · Nanaimo (best: ferry + SkyTrain) · Victoria (best: bus + ferry)
