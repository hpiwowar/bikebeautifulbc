Through the East Kootenays

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

59 cycling stops along this route · Kootenay Rockies

252 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelSome climbing

A 252 km bikepacking loop through the East Kootenays, following a modified version of the BC Trail to start and finish in Fernie. Mixed gravel and pavement with moderate climbing. The riders documented the trip in a beautifully shot ten-minute film, worth watching before you go.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on bikepacking.com.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
