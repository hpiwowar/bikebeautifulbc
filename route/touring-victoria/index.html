Touring Victoria

A ride mapped and described by Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

310 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

84 km · Full dayAmbitiousCalm streets, some sharingMostly pavedGentle grades

An 84 km rolling day ride through Greater Victoria, mostly paved, sampling the capital region's shoreline drives, quiet neighbourhoods, and rural edges with only about 400 m of climbing. A club-style tour that shows how much variety hides within an afternoon of the city.
