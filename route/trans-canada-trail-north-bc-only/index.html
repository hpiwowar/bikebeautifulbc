How we describe routes →

The far-north leg of the Trans Canada Trail in BC, about 1,028 km following the Alaska Highway corridor from Dawson Creek toward the Yukon. Highway riding, big country, long distances between towns and very remote: plan your stops and resupply with care.

☕Stops worth stopping for162 along this route

Fort Nelson Heritage Museum: Alaska Highway museum at Mile 300; WWII construction equipment across eight buildings. · Fort Nelson

Liard Hot Springs Lodge: second largest natural hot springs in Canada... soaking pools that run 42–52°C year-round. · Liard River

Liard River Hot Springs Campground: Canada's second-largest hot spring — a geothermal pool in lush boreal forest. · Fort Nelson

Muncho Lake: one of the most important resupply and rest stops along this stretch of northern highway; wood-fired sauna. · Fort Nelson

Northern Rockies Lodge: rare concentration of services in one of northern BC's most remote corridors. · Muncho Lake

Fast-road stretches#

We believe these are the fast, unprotected roads on this route. We can tell you which roads and roughly how far, but not the order you will ride them. If you see an error, tell us and we'll fix it.

  • about 899 km of Alaska Highway · near Fireside
  • about 24 km of Rolla Road · near Doe River
  • about 3 km of Dawson Creek-Tupper Highway · near Iracard
  • about 1 km of BC 97 · near Dawson Creek
  • short stretches on other roads · 22 km total
🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Beaver, Dene Tha’, Dënéndeh, Kaska Dena Kayeh, Kelly Lake Metis Settlement Society, Michif Piyii (Métis), Mountain Métis, and ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐤ ᐊᐢᑭᕀ Nêhiyaw-Askiy (Plains Cree).
