Trans Canada Trail North (BC Only)

A ride mapped and described by Trans Canada Trail

152 cycling stops along this route · Northern British Columbia

1031 km · ExpeditionEpicRide with trafficMostly pavedRemote stretches

The far-north leg of the Trans Canada Trail in BC, about 1,028 km following the Alaska Highway corridor from Dawson Creek toward the Yukon. Highway riding, big country, long distances between towns and very remote: plan your stops and resupply with care.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Trans Canada Trail.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
