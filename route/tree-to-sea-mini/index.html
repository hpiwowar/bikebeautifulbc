Tree To Sea Mini

A ride mapped and described by bikepacking.com

14 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

384 km · ExpeditionChallengingCalm streets, some sharingMostly gravelLots of climbingRemote stretches

The approachable version of the island's epic: a 384 km loop from Campbell River following much of the full Tree to Sea route while skipping the Tahsis-to-Zeballos boat charter and taking a more direct line to Gold River. Still a serious ride, mostly gravel with over 6,000 m of climbing and long remote stretches, but a fine introduction for riders short on time or working up to the full thousand.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on bikepacking.com.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
