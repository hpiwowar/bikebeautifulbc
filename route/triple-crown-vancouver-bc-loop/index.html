Triple Crown, Vancouver, BC (loop)

A ride mapped and described by The Great Community Route Celebration

324 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

112 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousRide with trafficMostly pavedMax climbing

The Triple Crown is Vancouver road cycling's rite of passage: climb the North Shore's three big paved ascents, Cypress, Grouse, and Seymour, in a single ride. This 112 km loop stays on the North Shore and routes you back to your start, collecting about 2,400 m of climbing along the way. All paved, all shared with traffic, and all reputation-earning.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on The Great Community Route Celebration.

This page covers what surrounds the route: welcoming stops, the communities along the way, and how to reach it.
