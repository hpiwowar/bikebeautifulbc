Triple Crown, Vancouver, BC (loop)

A ride described at The Great Community Route Celebration

How we describe routes →

The Triple Crown is Vancouver road cycling's rite of passage: climb the North Shore's three big paved ascents, Cypress, Grouse, and Seymour, in a single ride. This 112 km loop stays on the North Shore and routes you back to your start, collecting about 2,400 m of climbing along the way. All paved, all shared with traffic, and all reputation-earning.

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
