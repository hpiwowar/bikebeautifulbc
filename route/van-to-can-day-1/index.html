Van to Can Day 1

A ride described at Elbow Valley Cycle Club

How we describe routes →

Ambitious A real undertaking. Demanding on a couple of fronts, like rough surfaces plus steady climbing, or a long stretch on busier roads.

Difficulty Our overall read of how demanding a route is. We score surface roughness, climbing, singletrack, and how far it runs from services. The route publisher's own rating, a lot of busy road riding, or sheer scale can push the tier up, never down.

How we describe routes →

The opening day of the Elbow Valley Cycle Club's Vancouver-to-Canmore tour: 115 km of mostly paved, mostly flat riding east out of the city through the Fraser Valley, with about 800 m of gentle climbing. Straightforward touring with traffic for company.
