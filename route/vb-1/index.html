Seaside Beaches

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

18 km · Afternoon · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

EasyLow-traffic ridingMixed surface

Difficulty: Easy — no major climbing or rough surface.

Vancouver's beachfront path runs flat along the shore for 9 km one way, 18 return, hopping beach to beach with public art, sunset views, and swimming stops built in. Family-friendly the whole way, and busiest on exactly the days you most want to be there.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
