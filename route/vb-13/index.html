Vancouver to Steveston

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

44 km · Full day · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

AmbitiousLow-traffic ridingMixed surface

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

This out-and-back links Vancouver to Steveston along a former rail corridor and the Fraser River, 22 km each way, passing historic sites and open river views before arriving at the village's working fishing harbour. Flat the whole way; the 44 km round trip is the only difficulty.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Kwantlen, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), sc̓əwaθenaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsawwassen), Semiahmoo, Snuneymuxw, Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
