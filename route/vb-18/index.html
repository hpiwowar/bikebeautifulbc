BC Parkway

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

How we describe routes →

The BC Parkway follows the SkyTrain corridor for 18 km from Vancouver through Burnaby to New Westminster, flat and lined with pocket parks. With stations along the whole length, you can ride as far as you like and take the train back, one of the easiest one-way rides in the city.

Getting to this ride, bike and all#

Burnaby is the handiest transit doorway for this ride. The SkyTrain, ferries, and buses will carry you and your bike there:

  • New Westminsterbest: SkyTrain
  • Vancouverbest: SkyTrain
  • Nanaimobest: ferry + SkyTrain
  • Victoriabest: bus + ferry + SkyTrain

🪶Traditional territories

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, Kwantlen, kʷikʷəƛ̓əm, Qayqayt, Quw’utsun, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), sc̓əwaθenaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsawwassen), Semiahmoo, səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), sq̓əc̓iy̓aɁɬ təməxʷ (Katzie), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
