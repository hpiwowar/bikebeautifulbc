Seaside Greenway

A ride described in Let's Go Biking

24 km · Afternoon · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

ModerateLow-traffic ridingMostly paved

Difficulty: Moderate, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

The Seaside Greenway loops 24 km along Vancouver's waterfront, linking beaches, parks, and public art, with a tiny False Creek ferry stitching the shores together. Flat, family-friendly, and arguably the city's signature ride.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
