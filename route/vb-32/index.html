Seymour Valley

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

2 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

29 km · AfternoonAmbitiousComfortable for mostMixed surfaceChunky gravel

The Seymour Valley out-and-back runs 24 to 29 km through North Vancouver's deep forest, from the smooth, car-free paved parkway to rougher trail options near the lake and river. The easy line suits families; the trail sections turn rocky enough to want fatter tires. Views open up the valley as you go.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
