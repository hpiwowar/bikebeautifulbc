Shoreline Trail

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

68 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

9 km · AfternoonEasyComfortable for mostMostly paved

Port Moody's Shoreline Trail curves around the head of Burrard Inlet for 6 to 9 km of flat, family-friendly waterfront path, past mudflats busy with herons and parkland with plenty of stops. SkyTrain access makes it an easy car-free outing, and the brewery row nearby is no secret.

The route lives with its publisher
The full map, turn-by-turns, GPX, and the story behind this ride are on Let's Go Biking.
