Colony Farm

A ride mapped and described by Let's Go Biking

43 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

16 km · AfternoonEasyComfortable for mostMixed surface

Colony Farm Regional Park spreads flat over old farmland along the Coquitlam River, with 10 km of riding (16 return) between Port Coquitlam and Coquitlam. Community gardens, open meadows, and some of the region's best accessible birdwatching make it a favourite slow ride for families.
