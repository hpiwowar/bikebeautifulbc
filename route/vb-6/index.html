How we describe routes →

Difficulty: Ambitious, because of the route publisher's own rating as a minimum.

Bike the Bridge loops 20 km between Vancouver and the North Shore along seaside paths and waterfront, crossing the inlet by bridge one way and returning by SeaBus. Flat, scenic, and family-friendly, with the harbour views doing most of the entertaining.

Traditional territories#

This route travels through the traditional territories of the Cayuse, Umatilla and Walla Walla, Hul’qumi’num Treaty Group, S’ólh Téméxw (Stó:lō), səl̓ilwətaɁɬ təməxʷ (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwú7mesh-ulh Temíx̱w (Squamish), Stz’uminus, and šxʷməθkʷəy̓əmaɁɬ təməxʷ (Musqueam).
