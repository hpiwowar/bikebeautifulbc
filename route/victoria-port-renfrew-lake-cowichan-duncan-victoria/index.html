Victoria - Port Renfrew - Lake Cowichan - Duncan - Victoria

A ride mapped and described by Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society

619 cycling stops along this route · Vancouver Island

264 km · Weekend or moreAmbitiousRide with trafficMostly pavedSome climbing

A classic South Island road tour: start at the Empress Hotel in downtown Victoria, ride the coast road to Port Renfrew, cross the island's interior to Lake Cowichan and Duncan, and return, 264 km over two or three days with nearly 3,000 m of climbing. Paved throughout, remote and lightly serviced on the west-coast leg, and about as varied as island road riding gets.
