Bikepacking the Sea to Sky Trail
BIKEPACKING.com · 101 km · Weekend or more
ChallengingSome traffic sharingFast-road sections · 14 kmMixed surfaceLots of singletrackLots of climbing

The Sea to Sky Trail runs 101 km from Squamish through Whistler to Pemberton, tracing a corridor the Coast Salish and Interior Salish peoples travelled long before highways. Nearly half is flowy singletrack, and the rest mixes doubletrack, gravel, and pavement past turquoise lakes, swimming holes, suspension bridges, and even an old train wreck. Climbs come short and punchy rather than long. Resupply is easy in all three towns, and public transit can carry you back to the start. Guide by Miles Arbour and Nathan Reimer.

British Columbia Randonneurs

cycling clubvisit site →
British Columbia Randonneurs Cycling Club is a long-distance cycling club with deep roots: randonneuring in BC dates to 1979, and the club was a founding member of Les Randonneurs Mondiaux, the world body for 1,200 km grand randonnées, in 1983. With regional chapters in the Lower Mainland, Vancouver Island, Southern Interior, and Peace Region, it organizes brevets (timed long-distance rides of 200 km and up), populaires (shorter introductory events), and permanents (self-paced routes available year-round), and publishes the routes on Ride with GPS. Its celebrated Rocky Mountain 1200 drew riders from around the world.
