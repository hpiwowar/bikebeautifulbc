The people who keep BC rideable

Home/Trail Organizations

Behind almost every trail in this province is a small society of volunteers who cut it, bridge it, sign it, and clear the deadfall each spring. 58 organizations, in six regions. Back the ones whose trails you love.

Donations go directly to each organization, not to the BC Cycle Tourism Society. Where you see “tax receipt,” the organization is a registered charity. Donations to the BC Cycle Tourism Society are also welcome, contact us for details.

Cariboo Chilcotin Coast#

A small but mighty crew keeping the Cariboo and coast connected.

Lynn StevensBarkerville, BC

Wells and Areas Trails Society

A handful of volunteers keeping the trails around Wells and Barkerville open for bikes, boots, and skis. Every membership dollar goes directly into trail maintenance, and at twenty dollars a year it might be the best deal in the Cariboo.

Big country, volunteer-run trails from Hazelton to Tumbler Ridge.

Abby CooperSmithers, BC
Featured steward

Cycle16 Trail Society

They're building a 12 km paved trail between Telkwa and Smithers, so everyone in the Bulkley Valley can ride between the towns without riding Highway 16. Construction is underway, and your donation helps finish the trail.

Rail trails and dry-hill networks through the Okanagan and Shuswap.

Robb ThompsonColdstream, BC
Featured steward

Friends of Okanagan Rail Trail

The volunteer friends behind 52 km of continuous rail trail from Kelowna's waterfront along Kalamalka Lake to Coldstream, one of the easiest and loveliest rides in BC. Donations fund trailheads, benches, and the trail's next improvements.

The Kootenays’ deep bench of valley and mountain trail societies.

Kari MedigNew Denver, BC
Featured steward

Slocan Valley Rail Trail Society

Fifty kilometres of gentle rail trail through the Slocan Valley, from Slocan Lake to the Kootenay River, kept rideable by volunteers who fix the culverts and sinkholes an old rail line throws at them. Donation boxes wait at every trailhead if you prefer to give as you ride.
