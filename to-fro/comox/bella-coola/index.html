Same place twice. You’re already there, so enjoy the ride.

Good news: ferries, rental cars and flights can carry you and your bike from Comox to Bella Coola.

For scale
As the crow flies326 km
Biking whole way539 km · 28 h 11 m
By car541 km · 13 h 38 m

We found two ways to get you there, ferries doing the heavy lifting.

Note: Most carriers carry only a handful of bikes, and summer weekends fill up fast. Reserve where you can. Where you can't, go early or have a backup plan.
