Same place twice. You’re already there, so enjoy the ride.

Good news: buses and trains can carry you and your bike from Kelowna to Port Coquitlam.

For scale
As the crow flies248 km
Biking whole way386 km · 28 h 36 m
By car373 km · 3 h 34 m

We found two ways to get you there.

Note: Most carriers carry only a handful of bikes, and summer weekends fill up fast. Reserve where you can. Where you can't, go early or have a backup plan.
Option one · three legs⚠ doesn't run every day — check the schedule
The coach takes care of the long stretch.
