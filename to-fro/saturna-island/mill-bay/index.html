Same place twice. You’re already there, so enjoy the ride.

Good news: buses and ferries can carry you and your bike from Saturna Island to Mill Bay.

For scale
As the crow flies31 km
Biking whole way59 km · 3 h 16 m
By car94 km · 2 h 08 m

We found three ways to get you there, ferries doing the heavy lifting.

Note: Most carriers carry only a handful of bikes, and summer weekends fill up fast. Reserve where you can. Where you can't, go early or have a backup plan.
