ʔapsčiik t̓ašii (Pacific Rim Multi-use Path)

Home/Trails/ʔapsčiik t̓ašii (Pacific Rim Multi-use Path)
54 kmVancouver Island125 stops

ʔapsčiik t̓ašii runs through Pacific Rim National Park Reserve between Tofino and Ucluelet, on the traditional territories of the Tla-o-qui-aht and Yuułuʔiłʔatḥ. The paved path winds through rainforest, with spur trails to the big beaches. Surf, sand, and cedar, all car-free.
