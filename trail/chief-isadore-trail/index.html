Chief Isadore Trail

Home/Trails/Chief Isadore Trail
44 kmKootenay Rockies8 stops

Named for the Ktunaxa chief, this gravel trail runs east from Cranbrook through open range and pine forest toward the Kootenay River. It has a wilder feel than its paved neighbours, with mountain views across the valley. Watch for deer, and carry water.
