Cowichan Valley Trail

Home/Trails/Cowichan Valley Trail
86 kmVancouver Island16 stops

This rail trail climbs gently through the forests and farmland of the Cowichan Valley, crossing the Kinsol Trestle, one of the tallest timber rail trestles anywhere. Between trestles you pass lakes, rivers, and the odd winery. It is quiet riding, mostly gravel, with towns spaced right for a multi-day trip.
