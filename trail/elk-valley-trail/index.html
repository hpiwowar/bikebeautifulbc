Elk Valley Trail

Home/Trails/Elk Valley Trail
128 kmKootenay Rockies42 stops

The Elk Valley Trail links Fernie, Sparwood, and Elkford through the Rocky Mountain landscape of southeastern BC. It mixes smooth pathway with gravel doubletrack, always with big peaks in view. Part of the Trans Canada Trail, and a fine spine for a Rockies riding trip.

  2. The Big Griz
  3. Great Northern Bikepacking Route, BC Part Two
