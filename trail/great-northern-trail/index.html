Kootenay Rockies

Great Northern Trail

Home/Trails/Great Northern Trail
73 kmKootenay Rockies10 stops

A rougher, quieter rail trail through the forest between Nelson and Salmo. Expect a backcountry feel: gravel, creeks, and long stretches with nobody around. Good tires and a sense of adventure recommended.
