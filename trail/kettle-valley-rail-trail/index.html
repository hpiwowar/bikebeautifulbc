Thompson Okanagan

Kettle Valley Rail Trail

Home/Trails/Kettle Valley Rail Trail
476 kmThompson Okanagan173 stops

The KVR is BC's most famous rail trail, hundreds of kilometres of railway grade winding through the Okanagan's hills, canyons, and vineyards. The Myra Canyon section alone crosses 18 wooden trestles high above Kelowna. Ride the Naramata Bench for wineries and lake views, or set out for days across the whole network.
