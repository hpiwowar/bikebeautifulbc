Nakusp & Slocan Rail Trail

Home/Trails/Nakusp & Slocan Rail Trail
74 kmKootenay Rockies4 stops

The old Nakusp & Slocan railway grade rolls through deep Kootenay forest between Nakusp and Summit Lake toward Rosebery. It is quiet, green, and lightly travelled. Pair it with a soak at Nakusp's hot springs.
