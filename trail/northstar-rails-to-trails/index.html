Kootenay Rockies

NorthStar Rails to Trails

Home/Trails/NorthStar Rails to Trails
26 kmKootenay Rockies9 stops

A smooth, paved rail trail between Cranbrook and Kimberley, gaining height gently the whole way to Kimberley's alpine-styled downtown. The Rockies stand along the eastern skyline for most of the ride. It suits every kind of rider, including kids.
