Okanagan Rail Trail

Home/Trails/Okanagan Rail Trail
49 kmThompson Okanagan110 stops

The Okanagan Rail Trail follows the old rail line from Kelowna to Coldstream, hugging the turquoise water of Kalamalka and Wood Lakes for much of the way. The grade is gentle and the surface is smooth. Pack swimwear; the lake access is hard to resist.
