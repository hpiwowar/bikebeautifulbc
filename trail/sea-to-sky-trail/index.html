Sea to Sky Trail

Home/Trails/Sea to Sky Trail
72 kmVancouver, Coast and Mountains228 stops

A work in progress that will one day link Squamish to D'Arcy; the sections around Squamish, Whistler, and the Cheakamus Canyon are already superb. Expect gravel, real climbs, and mountain scenery that earns the name. It is the wilder alternative to the highway.
