Vancouver, Coast and Mountains

Seaside Greenway

Home/Trails/Seaside Greenway
33 kmVancouver, Coast and Mountains983 stops

Vancouver's waterfront path runs from Coal Harbour around Stanley Park, along False Creek, and out past Kitsilano to Spanish Banks. It is one of the longest uninterrupted waterfront paths anywhere, and surely one of the prettiest. Beaches, street food, and city views the entire way.
