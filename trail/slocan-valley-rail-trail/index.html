Slocan Valley Rail Trail

Home/Trails/Slocan Valley Rail Trail
50 kmKootenay Rockies14 stops

The Slocan Valley Rail Trail follows its river for fifty easy kilometres between South Slocan and Slocan City. The water is never far: green pools, gravel bars, and mountain views the whole way. Villages like Winlaw supply coffee and baked goods at just the right intervals.
