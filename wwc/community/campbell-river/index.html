Routes around Campbell River#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Campbell River or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Tree to Sea Loop, Vancouver Island — BIKEPACKING.com · 1,046 km route, 45 km of it through Campbell River
  2. Campbell River — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 32 km, all of it in Campbell River
  3. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 28 km of it through Campbell River
  4. Cross Canada - Far North - Vancouver Island — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 498 km route, 26 km of it through Campbell River
  5. Snowden or Dust, Vancouver Island — BIKEPACKING.com · 75 km route, 11 km of it through Campbell River
  6. 4 Ferry Odyssey — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 165 km route, 11 km of it through Campbell River
  7. Elk Falls — Let's Go Biking · 100 km route, 9 km of it through Campbell River
  8. Frog Greenways Loop — Let's Go Biking · 28 km route, 7 km of it through Campbell River
  9. Quadra Island — Let's Go Biking · 30 km ride, passing about 2 km away
