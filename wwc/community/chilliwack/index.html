Routes around Chilliwack#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Chilliwack or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 38 km of it through Chilliwack
  2. Vedder Sumas River — Trails BC · 92 km route, 35 km of it through Chilliwack
  3. Cross Canada - Vancouver to Hope — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 176 km route, 32 km of it through Chilliwack
  4. Canyon to Coast Trail Loop — Let's Go Biking · 34 km route, 31 km of it through Chilliwack
  5. BC Gravel (BC Only) — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 1,300 km route, 30 km of it through Chilliwack
  6. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, 29 km of it through Chilliwack
  7. Vedder Loop — Let's Go Biking · 20 km, all of it in Chilliwack
  8. Rotary Loop Trail — Trails BC · 12 km, all of it in Chilliwack
  9. Van to Can Day 1 — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 116 km route, 9 km of it through Chilliwack
  10. Maple Leaf Populaire — British Columbia Randonneurs · 159 km route, 7 km of it through Chilliwack
