Routes around Denman Island#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides pass close to Denman Island, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Qualicum Bay - Royston - Denman Island — Let's Go Biking · 77 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  2. Sunshine Coast Tour Day 5 — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 110 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  3. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, passing about 2 km away
  4. Cross Canada - Far North - Vancouver Island — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 498 km route, passing about 3 km away
