  3. Cowichan Valley Trail — Let's Go Biking · 88 km route, 2 km of it through Duncan
  4. Victoria - Port Renfrew - Lake Cowichan - Duncan - Victoria — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 264 km route, 1 km of it through Duncan
  5. Duncan — Let's Go Biking · 20 km route, 1 km of it through Duncan
  6. Cowichan Valley 8 Bikepacking Route — bikepacking.com · 190 km route, 1 km of it through Duncan
  7. Cross Canada - Far North - Vancouver Island — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 498 km route, 1 km of it through Duncan

  10. Rally Day 1 (West Vancouver) — Gravel Buddies · 160 km route, passing about 1 km away
