Routes around Mission#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Mission or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 34 km of it through Mission
  2. Maple Leaf Populaire — British Columbia Randonneurs · 159 km route, passing within a kilometre
  3. Langley to Clayburn — Trails BC · 28 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  4. Roller Coaster — Let's Go Biking · 47 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  5. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, passing within a kilometre
  6. Matsqui Meander — Let's Go Biking · 42 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  7. Glen Valley Glide — Let's Go Biking · 42 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  8. Cross Canada - Vancouver to Hope — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 176 km route, passing about 1 km away
  9. Golden Ears Overnighter (out and back) — Gravel Buddies · 105 km ride, passing about 3 km away
  10. BC Gravel (BC Only) — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 1,300 km route, passing about 4 km away
