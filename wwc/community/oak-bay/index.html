Routes around Oak Bay#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Oak Bay or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Seaside Loop — Let's Go Biking · 40 km route, 7 km of it through Oak Bay
  2. Touring Victoria — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 84 km route, 6 km of it through Oak Bay
  3. Metchosin Caper — Bike Gear Database · 83 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  4. Vancouver Crossing Loop — Adventure Cycling Association · 503 km route, passing about 1 km away
  5. Victoria — Let's Go Biking · 20 km ride, passing about 1 km away
  6. Saanich Centennial Trails — Let's Go Biking · 45 km ride, passing about 2 km away
  7. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, passing about 2 km away
  8. BT700 Port Renfrew — BT 700 Bikepacking · 174 km route, passing about 3 km away
  9. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, passing about 3 km away
  10. Barry's Loop — Bike Gear Database · 58 km ride, passing about 3 km away

We’re showing the 10 that cover the most ground here. Another 12 come through or near Oak Bay. See every ride in Vancouver Island →
