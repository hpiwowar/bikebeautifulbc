Routes around Parksville#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Parksville or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Englishman River Overnighter — BIKEPACKING.com · 52 km route, 21 km of it through Parksville
  2. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 14 km of it through Parksville
  3. Rathtrevor Park - Top Bridge Trail — Let's Go Biking · 30 km route, 14 km of it through Parksville
  4. Parksville - Qualicum Beach — Let's Go Biking · 32 km route, 12 km of it through Parksville
  5. Sunshine Coast Tour Day 5 — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 110 km route, 9 km of it through Parksville
  6. Nanoose Bay - Parksville — Let's Go Biking · 40 km route, 8 km of it through Parksville
  7. Cross Canada - Far North - Vancouver Island — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 498 km route, 2 km of it through Parksville
  8. Alberni Bam Bam, Vancouver Island — BIKEPACKING.com · 507 km route, passing about 3 km away
