Routes around Penticton#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Penticton or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Penticton Exploring — Let's Go Biking · 20 km route, 12 km of it through Penticton
  2. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, 12 km of it through Penticton
  3. BC Gravel (BC Only) — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 1,300 km route, 10 km of it through Penticton
  4. The BC Trail — BIKEPACKING.com · 1,164 km route, 10 km of it through Penticton
  5. Great Northern Bikepacking Route, BC Part Two — Great Northern Bikepacking Route · 1,187 km route, 10 km of it through Penticton
  6. Naramata — Let's Go Biking · 37 km route, 9 km of it through Penticton
  7. KVR - Penticton to Midway — Let's Go Biking · 214 km route, 7 km of it through Penticton
  8. Skaha Lake — Let's Go Biking · 30 km route, 4 km of it through Penticton
  9. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 3 km of it through Penticton
  10. Summerland Cycle Loop — Trails BC · 54 km route, just clipping the edge of Penticton
