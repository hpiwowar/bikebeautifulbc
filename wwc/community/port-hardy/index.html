Routes around Port Hardy#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Port Hardy or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Port Hardy — Let's Go Biking · 26 km route, 20 km of it through Port Hardy
  2. Tree to Sea Loop, Vancouver Island — bikepacking.com · 1,046 km route, 15 km of it through Port Hardy
  3. Cross Canada - Far North - Vancouver Island — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 498 km route, 9 km of it through Port Hardy
  4. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 6 km of it through Port Hardy
