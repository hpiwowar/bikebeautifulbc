Routes around Princeton#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Princeton or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 6 km of it through Princeton
  2. Kettle Valley Rail Trail - Hope to Summerland — Let's Go Biking · 254 km route, 6 km of it through Princeton
  3. BC Gravel (BC Only) — Elbow Valley Cycle Club · 1,300 km route, 5 km of it through Princeton
  4. Great Northern Bikepacking Route, BC Part Two — Great Northern Bikepacking Route · 1,187 km route, 5 km of it through Princeton
  5. The BC Trail — BIKEPACKING.com · 1,164 km route, 5 km of it through Princeton
  6. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, 4 km of it through Princeton
