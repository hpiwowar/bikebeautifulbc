Routes around Sidney#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through Sidney or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. Touring Victoria — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 84 km route, 9 km of it through Sidney
  2. North Saanich — Let's Go Biking · 60 km route, 8 km of it through Sidney
  3. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 7 km of it through Sidney
  4. Salish Sea Trail — Let's Go Biking · 175 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  5. Lochside Trail — Let's Go Biking · 29 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  6. Seaside Loop — Let's Go Biking · 40 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  7. Saanich Centennial Trails — Let's Go Biking · 45 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  8. Sooke Potholes (out and back) — Gravel Buddies · 158 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  9. Vancouver Crossing Loop — Adventure Cycling Association · 503 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney
  10. Cowichan Valley: Swartz Bay to Stoltz Pool — Gravel Buddies · 83 km route, 4 km of it through Sidney

We’re showing the 10 that cover the most ground here. Another 2 come through or near Sidney. See every ride in Vancouver Island →
