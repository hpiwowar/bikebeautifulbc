Routes around University Hill#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides pass close to University Hill, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. UBC Explorer — Let's Go Biking · 9 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  2. Vancouver Crossing Loop — Adventure Cycling Association · 503 km route, passing within a kilometre
  3. Pacific Spirit Park — Let's Go Biking · 16 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  4. Cherry Blossom Populaire — British Columbia Randonneurs · 102 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  5. Seaside Beaches — Let's Go Biking · 18 km ride, passing about 1 km away
  6. Seaside Greenway — Let's Go Biking · 24 km ride, passing about 1 km away
