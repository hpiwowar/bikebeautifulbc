Routes around West Vancouver#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through West Vancouver or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  3. Trans Canada Trail South (BC Only) — Trans Canada Trail · 1,768 km route, 23 km of it through West Vancouver
  4. Great Northern Bikepacking Route, BC Part One — Great Northern Bikepacking Route · 583 km route, 21 km of it through West Vancouver
  5. Vancouver Crossing Loop — Adventure Cycling Association · 503 km route, 20 km of it through West Vancouver
  6. Rally Day 1 (West Vancouver) — Gravel Buddies · 160 km route, 16 km of it through West Vancouver
  7. Rally Day 1 alternative (West Vancouver) — Gravel Buddies · 150 km route, 16 km of it through West Vancouver
  8. North Shore through Stanley Park — Gravel Buddies · 37 km route, 10 km of it through West Vancouver
  9. Trans Canada Bike Route (BC Only) — Trans Canada Bike Route Network · 5,446 km route, 10 km of it through West Vancouver
  10. Capilano Salmon Run — Gravel Buddies · 20 km route, 9 km of it through West Vancouver
