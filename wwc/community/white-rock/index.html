Routes around White Rock#

You don’t have to plan from scratch. These rides run through White Rock or pass close by, mapped and written up by route publishers around BC. The full maps and turn-by-turns live on their sites.

  1. White Rock + South Surrey Big Loop — Let's Go Biking · 32 km route, 6 km of it through White Rock
  2. Friesen Loop White Rock to Crescent Beach — Let's Go Biking · 24 km route, 4 km of it through White Rock
  3. Friesens Ride — Let's Go Biking · 24 km route, 1 km of it through White Rock
  4. South Surrey Trails — Let's Go Biking · 25 km route, just clipping the edge of White Rock
  5. Bakerview to Fort Langley — Cross Canada Cycle Tour Society · 84 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  6. Semiahmoo Trail — Let's Go Biking · 4 km ride, passing within a kilometre
  7. ACA Pacific Coast (BC Only) — Adventure Cycling Association · 65 km ride, passing about 2 km away
  8. Surrey Shores — Let's Go Biking · 12 km ride, passing about 3 km away
Getting there with a bike: province-wide buses, ferries, and shuttles are listed under Transport (some serving White Rock may be listed there rather than here).
