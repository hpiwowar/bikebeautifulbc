Accommodation guide for cyclists in British Columbia

For touring cyclists, accommodation is more than a place to sleep. The most important question is often simple: where will the bike go overnight? This guide highlights accommodation that may be useful to cyclists travelling in British Columbia, from hotels and motels to hostels, inns, B&Bs, guesthouses, and lodges.

A good cyclist-friendly stay makes arrival easy after a long day on the road or trail. Secure overnight bike storage, a clear bike procedure, laundry or drying options, charging access, nearby food, and staff who understand loaded bikes can make the difference between a stressful stop and a good one.

What makes an accommodation stop welcoming for cyclists?#

Core criteria

  • Secure overnight bike storage

Helpful extras we track for accommodation stops

  • Bike parking details: Bikes in rooms
  • Food & water: Early or packed breakfast, Nearby evening food, Nearby grocery, Vending machines
  • Charging: E-bike charging, Device charging
  • Gear & comfort: Laundry, Drying area, Luggage storage, Wi-Fi
  • Booking & boarding: Min-stay waived
  • Bike care: Pump, Basic tools, Bike wash / hose
