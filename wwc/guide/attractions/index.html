Attractions guide for cyclists in British Columbia

Cycle tourism is not only about moving between overnight stops. The best rides also include places to pause, learn, refill, warm up, cool down, meet people, and understand the communities and landscapes you are travelling through.

This guide highlights attractions and community places that may be useful or interesting to cyclists in British Columbia, including visitor centres, museums, heritage sites, libraries, community centres, hot springs, galleries, and cultural stops. Practical details matter here too: bike parking, water, washrooms, opening hours, admission requirements, and whether a stop is easy to visit by bike can all affect whether it belongs in a cycling itinerary.

Use this page to understand what kinds of attractions appear in the directory, then open the interactive directory to explore stops by community, category, or route.

What makes an attractions stop welcoming for cyclists?#

Core criteria

  • Bike parking
  • Water bottle refill
  • Washrooms

Helpful extras we track for attractions stops

  • Bike parking details: Bike rack visible from inside, Covered bike parking, Public rack nearby, Bike allowed inside/on patio
  • Washrooms & shower details: Showers (drop-in or fee)
  • Food & water: Café or food on site, Water without admission, Vending machines
  • Charging: Device charging, E-bike charging
  • Gear & comfort: Luggage / bag storage, Wi-Fi
  • Booking & boarding: Discount for arriving by bike
  • Bike care: Pump
