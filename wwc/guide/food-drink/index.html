Food & Drink guide for cyclists in British Columbia

Food and drink stops matter differently when you are travelling by bike. A café, bakery, pub, grocery stop, winery, brewery, or rural restaurant may be a meal, a water refill, a washroom, a weather break, a charging stop, or the only reliable service for many kilometres.

This guide highlights food and drink listings that may be useful to cyclists travelling in British Columbia. We look for practical details that help riders decide whether a stop will work during a ride, such as bike parking or bike visibility, water bottle refills, washrooms, opening hours, and whether a loaded bike can be kept reasonably secure while you stop.

Use this page to understand how food and drink listings fit into the BC Bike Stops directory, then open the interactive directory to search by place, category, or route.

What makes a food & drink stop welcoming for cyclists?#

Core criteria

  • Bike parking or bike visibility
  • Water bottle refill
  • Washrooms

Helpful extras we track for food & drink stops

  • Bike parking details: Bike rack visible from inside, Covered bike parking, Public rack nearby, Bike allowed inside/on patio
  • Food & water: Takeout, Packed lunches, Water without purchase, Vegetarian / vegan / GF
  • Charging: Device charging, E-bike charging
  • Gear & comfort: Wi-Fi
  • Bike care: Pump
