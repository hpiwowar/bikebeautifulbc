Sushiway

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Port Coquitlam$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Sushiway is a Japanese restaurant in Port Coquitlam featuring an open-kitchen concept where diners can watch chefs steam, chop, and stir fry. The menu spans sushi, sashimi, katsu, and Japanese comfort food alongside vegetarian staples like agedashi tofu and edamame. Online ordering is available for convenient takeout.
