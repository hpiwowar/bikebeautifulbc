Fleetwood Farm Market

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Farmers' Market📍 Surrey
Basic groceries

Fleetwood Farm Market is a neighbourhood supermarket-style farm market on 152 Street in the Fleetwood area of Surrey. Its farm market branding suggests a focus on fresh produce and local goods alongside general grocery staples. Cyclists passing through the Fleetwood corridor will find it a practical resupply point for food and provisions.
