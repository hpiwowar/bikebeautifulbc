Doughgirls

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Bakery / Donuts📍 University Hill$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

Doughgirls is a neighbourhood bakeshop and comfort kitchen in Wesbrook Village, founded by two culinary professionals with decades of combined experience. The shop turns out artisan breads baked fresh daily, handcrafted comfort foods made with local and sustainable ingredients, and take-n-bake meals including savoury pies and seasonal soups. A large reclaimed fir communal table anchors the welcoming dining room, making it a practical and convivial stop for fresh food.
