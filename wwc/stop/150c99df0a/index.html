Milestones Grill & Bar is a well-established Canadian casual dining chain located at Tsawwassen Mills, near the Tsawwassen ferry terminal and the South Delta cycling network. The restaurant offers a full sit-down menu with bar service, accommodating dietary needs including vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options. Its proximity to the BC Ferries terminal makes it a logical stop for cyclists arriving from or departing to Vancouver Island or the Southern Gulf Islands.
