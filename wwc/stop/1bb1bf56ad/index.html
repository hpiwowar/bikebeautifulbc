P2B Restaurant + Bar

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Vancouver$$$ · Upscale
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

P2B Restaurant + Bar is a West Coast-inspired full-service restaurant and bar located inside the Pinnacle Hotel Harbourfront in Downtown Vancouver's Coal Harbour neighbourhood, with views of the mountains and the waterfront cityscape. The menu celebrates BC's best — Ocean Wise seafood, Fraser Valley produce, and artisanal meats — across a daily spread from breakfast through dinner and a lively happy hour. Private dining, a full bar, and regular live music make it a gathering spot for both business travellers and tourists exploring the nearby seawall.
