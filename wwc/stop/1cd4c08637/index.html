Deacon's Corner

Deacon's Corner is a southern-style comfort food restaurant that has anchored the Gastown neighbourhood since 2009, inspired by a truck stop diner on the Trans-Canada Highway outside Winnipeg. The menu leans heavily on hearty all-day breakfast dishes — fried chicken, eggs every style, biscuits, hash bowls, and bennys — made in-house from scratch with generous portions. The restaurant describes itself as a 'perfect pit stop for explorers,' and its brunch-focused format suits a mid-ride refuel well.
