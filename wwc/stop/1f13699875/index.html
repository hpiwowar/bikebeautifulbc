Brix + Mortar Socialhouse

Brix + Mortar SocialHouse is a locally owned cafe, bakery, and social gathering spot in the heart of Downtown Nanaimo, one block from the historic Nanaimo Bastion. Opened in 2025 by founder Leon, it serves barista-style espresso drinks, fresh-baked croissants, grilled paninis, waffles, soups, and locally sourced pasta in a custom-built interior designed to evoke the spirit of Vancouver Island. Both dine-in and takeaway are available, making it a convenient stop for cyclists passing through the downtown waterfront area.
