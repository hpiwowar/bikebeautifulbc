Dae Bak Bon Ga

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Vancouver$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutPublic rack nearby

Dae Bak Bon Ga is a long-running Korean BBQ restaurant on Vancouver's busy Robson Street, serving both à la carte and all-you-can-eat BBQ menus with over 140 seats for small and large groups. The restaurant has operated for more than 20 years and also features eight private karaoke rooms available for booking. It is located on the second floor (unit 201) of its building, presented by Mirak Foods Ltd.
