Leon's Cafe

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Leon's Cafe is a Belgian-inspired coffee shop in Surrey specializing in traditional Belgian waffles, freshly brewed coffee, and locally sourced sandwiches and treats. The café cultivates a distinctly European atmosphere and offers both indoor and outdoor seating. It also hosts community events including art exhibitions and bingo days, making it a lively neighbourhood stop.
