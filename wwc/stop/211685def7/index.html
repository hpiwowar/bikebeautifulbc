Brewster's Sports Pub & Liquor Store

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Brewery / Pub📍 Surrey$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Brewster's Sports Pub is a neighbourhood sports bar offering hearty pub fare alongside a broad selection of draught and packaged beer, wine, and cocktails. The pub features live sports viewing, darts, billiards, table soccer, and live music events, giving it a lively, multi-activity atmosphere. A touring cyclist would find a full sit-down menu with several vegetarian options plus an online ordering option for takeout.
