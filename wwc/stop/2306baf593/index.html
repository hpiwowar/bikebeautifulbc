Princess and the Pea Bed & Breakfast

InfoBuy BC (independent) · B&B / Inn📍 Langley$$$ · Mid-range

Princess and the Pea Bed & Breakfast is a small, owner-operated B&B in Langley, BC, run by Sharon & Wally Martin. Its storybook name hints at a cozy, attentive hosting style typical of independent B&Bs in the Fraser Valley. Touring cyclists riding through Langley on routes connecting the Lower Mainland to the Interior would find it a locally rooted alternative to chain accommodation.
