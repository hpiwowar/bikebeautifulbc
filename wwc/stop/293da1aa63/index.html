Lucky Bar

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Brewery / Pub📍 Victoria$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

Lucky Bar is a storied live-music and DJ venue in downtown Victoria, programming an eclectic range of genres — punk, metal, hip-hop, house, reggae, folk, jazz, and more — alongside variety shows and film screenings under the motto 'Everything happens at Lucky Bar.' Situated on Yates Street, it runs multiple events most nights of the week and draws a devoted local crowd. For touring cyclists, it offers an accessible evening entertainment option right in the heart of the city.
