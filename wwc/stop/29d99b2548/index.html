Derby Reach Regional Park

Info🏛️ Public · Park / Nature📍 LangleyFree

Derby Reach Regional Park is a Metro Vancouver regional park along the Fraser River in Langley, featuring trails through forest, wetland, and heritage farmland. The park sits on the Fraser River floodplain and offers a mix of riverside and upland walking and cycling paths. It is a pleasant stop for touring cyclists riding through the Lower Mainland's eastern communities.
