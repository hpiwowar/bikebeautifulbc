Jimmy Macs Liquor Store

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Liquor Store📍 Langley

Jimmy Macs Liquor Store is the retail liquor arm of Jimy Mac's Neighbourhood Pub, a full-service neighbourhood pub in Langley, BC. The adjoining pub serves a broad menu spanning weekend brunch, burgers, appetizers, and a wide selection of draught beers, wines, and cocktails. A touring cyclist stopping here can resupply on beverages and step next door into the pub for a sit-down meal.
