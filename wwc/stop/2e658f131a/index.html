Schoolhouse Farm

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Farmers' Market📍 Delta
Basic groceries

Schoolhouse Farm is a small, woman-owned, family-run diversified farm in Ladner operating with no-spray, no-till, regenerative practices. Its farm store carries seasonally harvested vegetables, culinary and medicinal herbs, and cut flowers, plus a curated selection of third-party goods, accepting cash, card, and e-transfer. When fields are blooming, the farm also offers u-pick flower sessions in a working farm setting framed by willow trees and public seating.
