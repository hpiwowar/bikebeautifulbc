Golden Panda Restaurant is a Chinese restaurant located in Surrey, BC, offering dine-in as well as online ordering for pickup and delivery within a 4-mile radius. The website presents a straightforward neighbourhood restaurant with a focus on takeout convenience. A touring cyclist passing through the area would find it a practical meal stop, though cycling-specific amenities are not advertised.
