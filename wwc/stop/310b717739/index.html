Golden Sweets & Restaurant

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Golden Sweets & Restaurant is an authentic Indian cuisine spot in Surrey specializing in butter chicken, Tandoori items, traditional thali, and Indian sweets made fresh daily. The menu spans a wide range of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes including Indian street food classics like Gol Gappe, Chaat Papri, and assorted pakoras. A touring cyclist looking for a filling, flavourful sit-down meal or a quick takeout stop will find a broad menu with something for most dietary preferences.
