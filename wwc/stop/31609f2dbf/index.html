Chào Mámì

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Burnaby$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Chào Mámì ('Hello Mom') is a Vietnamese restaurant in Burnaby Heights that grew out of a previous venture called Brokenrice, built around celebrating the owner's mother's cooking with fresh, vibrant ingredients and a contemporary approach to classic Vietnamese dishes. The menu spans small plates, specialty noodle soups including pho, and a full bar with Vietnamese-inspired cocktails and imported Asian beers. It operates as a full-service dine-in restaurant with delivery available through major platforms.
