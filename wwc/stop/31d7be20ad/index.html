Hanaya Japanese Restaurant

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Hanaya Japanese Restaurant is a Japanese sushi and dining restaurant in Surrey, BC, in operation since at least 2004. It offers dine-in, online ordering, and delivery, making it a practical stop for cyclists looking for a full sit-down Japanese meal. The restaurant is wheelchair accessible and accepts credit and debit cards.
