All About Pho

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutPublic rack nearby

All About Pho is a Vietnamese restaurant in Surrey serving traditional pho, vermicelli bowls, banh mi, bubble tea, and a broad range of Vietnamese favourites prepared with fresh organic ingredients and family recipes. The restaurant emphasises cleanliness and friendly service and has drawn a loyal local following. Both dine-in and takeout are available, making it a practical stop for a quick, filling meal.
