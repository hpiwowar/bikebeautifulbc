Accent Inns Burnaby

✶ AccreditedBuy BC (chain) · Hotel / Motel📍 Burnaby$$$ · Mid-range
Secure overnight bike storageLaundryNearby evening foodBikes in roomsBasic toolsBike wash / hoseWi-Fi

Accent Inns Burnaby is a BC family-owned hotel close to the Gilmore SkyTrain station, positioning itself as one of the most bike-friendly lodging options in Canada with complimentary bike rentals available to guests. The property is Rainbow Registered, holds BC Hotel Association housekeeping awards, and hosts the locally operated Bin 4 Burger Lounge on site. Its accessible location off Highway 1 and proximity to transit makes it a practical base for cyclists exploring the Greater Vancouver area.
