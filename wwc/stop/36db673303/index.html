Notti Biscotti

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Bakery / Donuts📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

Notti Biscotti is a South Surrey artisan bakery and café founded by Shelley Quesnel, specializing in over twenty flavours of thick, chocolate-based Italian biscotti made with all-natural, preservative-free ingredients. Located at Morgan Creek Corporate Centre, the café also serves muffins, scones, Notti Squares, brownies, and a fresh lunch menu including soups. The business won the Surrey Board of Trade Business Excellence Award in 2007 and has expanded to over 50 retail outlets across the region.
