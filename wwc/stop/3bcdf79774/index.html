The Federal Store

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Vancouver$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

The Federal Store is a small neighbourhood grocery and cafe in the heart of Mount Pleasant, Vancouver, specializing in local groceries, lunches, handmade baking, artisan cakes, and specialty coffee. It carries a curated selection of locally made products and offers an online shop for custom cake orders with pick-up or delivery. A touring cyclist passing through Mount Pleasant would find it a solid stop for a meal, coffee, and locally sourced provisions.
