Pho Fresh

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Phở Fresh is a family-owned Northern Vietnamese restaurant in Tsawwassen, Delta, founded in 2018 by a Vietnamese-Canadian engineer who spent 15+ hours daily simmering scratch-made bone broth in the Hanoi tradition. The clean, modern dining room is designed for a comfortable sit-down meal, and the restaurant also caters events and offers delivery through major apps. A touring cyclist stopping here will find a restorative, allergen-conscious bowl of phở made entirely from local BC ingredients where practical.
