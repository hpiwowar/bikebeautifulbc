Tim Hortons

WelcomingGlobal chain · Fast Food / Chain📍 Chilliwack$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutPublic rack nearbyWi-Fi

Tim Hortons is Canada's ubiquitous quick-service coffee and food chain, and this Chilliwack location on Vedder Road offers both dine-in and drive-thru service. The menu spans freshly brewed coffee, soups, wraps, baked goods, and Timbits, making it a practical rest stop for cyclists needing a hot meal or a caffeine fix. The location is wheelchair accessible and includes free Wi-Fi.
