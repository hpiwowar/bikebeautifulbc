Ton Ton Sushi

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Vancouver$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutCovered bike parkingPublic rack nearby

Ton Ton Sushi is a Japanese and Korean sushi restaurant on Cambie Street in Vancouver, serving sushi and a fusion of Japanese and Korean dishes. The restaurant offers online ordering through their website and a dedicated app. Located along the Cambie Street corridor near the Canada Line, it is a convenient stop for cyclists moving through South Vancouver.
