Holiday Park Resort

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Hotel / Motel📍 Okanagan Falls$$ · Budget
Nearby evening foodNearby grocery

Holiday Park Resort is a sprawling 67-acre RV and vacation condo resort situated between Kelowna and Vernon in Lake Country, BC, featuring 570 RV sites and 117 vacation condos. Founded in 1983 and open year-round, the resort is surrounded by championship golf courses, award-winning wineries, and ski hills. An on-site convenience store and bike rentals make it a practical staging point for touring cyclists exploring the Okanagan.
