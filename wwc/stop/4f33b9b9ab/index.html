Maple Leaf Indian Cuisine

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Maple Ridge$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GF

Maple Leaf Indian Cuisine is a family-owned Indian restaurant in Maple Ridge serving fresh, homemade curries and tandoori dishes made with authentic Indian spices. The space features casual dining alongside a contemporary bar that doubles as an event venue for parties and celebrations. The restaurant operates a second location in Surrey, suggesting an established local presence.
