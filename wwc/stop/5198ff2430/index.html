Little Pisces Taiyaki & Soft Serve

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Ice Cream📍 Burnaby$ · Cheap eats
TakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Little Pisces is a Japanese-inspired dessert shop serving mochi-batter taiyaki — crispy on the outside, soft and chewy on the inside — alongside soft serve ice cream and specialty café drinks. The shop opened its first location in June 2024 on the second floor of a Lougheed-area plaza in Burnaby, with a rotating menu of creative flavors and a strong community following. A good stop for a sweet break, with pickup and delivery available.
