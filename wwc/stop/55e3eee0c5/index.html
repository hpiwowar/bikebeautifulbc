Liberty Kitchen

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Langford$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashrooms

Liberty Kitchen is a Canadian neighbourhood restaurant chain (part of Browns Restaurant Group) serving made-from-scratch meals in a community-focused setting. The Langford (Belmont Market) outpost sits in a busy retail and dining complex on the western edge of Greater Victoria. Touring cyclists passing through the Capital Region would find it a convenient full-service meal stop with a full dining room.
