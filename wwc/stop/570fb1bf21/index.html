Honeybee Centre

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Honeybee Centre is Western Canada's top commercial honey farm, operating a publicly accessible Country Store in Surrey where visitors can browse and purchase locally produced specialty honeys, bee pollen, propolis, royal jelly, and beeswax candles. Cyclists can stop in to shop and watch honeybees at work in the back of the store. The centre also runs educational tours, workshops, and field trips, making it a genuinely distinctive stop on any Lower Mainland cycling route.
