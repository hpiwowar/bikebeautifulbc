Beatniks Bistro

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Langley$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

Beatniks Bistro occupies one of Fort Langley's oldest buildings, a 1933 heritage house originally built as the Moorhouse family residence, giving the space a warm, lived-in character distinct from purpose-built restaurants. The bistro serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner alongside a seasonal patio recognized as one of the top patios in the Fraser Valley. Live music every Thursday evening makes it a particularly lively stop for cyclists riding through the historic village of Fort Langley.
