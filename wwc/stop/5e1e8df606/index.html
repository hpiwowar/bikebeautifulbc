The Landing Liquor Store

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Liquor Store📍 Nanaimo
Public rack nearby

The Landing Liquor Store is Nanaimo's largest private liquor retailer, housed in a spacious unit at the Brooks Landing shopping centre on Island Highway North. The store carries over 5,000 products including 750+ craft beers, 400 BC VQA wines, and 300+ scotch varieties, with an on-site sommelier available for consultation. A rentable private tasting room with a bar, projector, and food service makes it a notable stop for groups looking to explore BC's wine and craft beer scene.
