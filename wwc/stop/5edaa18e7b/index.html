Forever Bikes

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Repair Shop📍 Chilliwack
Pump availableE-bike service

Forever Bikes, also trading as Jack's Cycle, has served Chilliwack cyclists since 1963, making it one of the longest-running bike shops in the Fraser Valley. It is a full-service shop carrying a wide range from BMX and mountain to commuter and high-end e-bikes across brands like Specialized, Gazelle, CUBE, Ibis, and Knolly. A professional repair department offers free estimates and handles everything from minor adjustments to full overhauls, with a broad accessories inventory covering racks, lighting, locks, hydration, and tires.
