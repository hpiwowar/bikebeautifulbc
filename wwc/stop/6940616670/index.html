Starbucks

InfoGlobal chain · Fast Food / Chain📍 Richmond$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutWi-Fi

Starbucks is a chain coffee shop offering espresso drinks and light food items. Located on Sea Island Way in Richmond, it serves as a convenient stop for touring cyclists passing through the area. This location provides the familiar, reliable service expected from the Starbucks brand.
