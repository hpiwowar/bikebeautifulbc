De Dutch

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Beach Grove$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GF

De Dutch is a long-established Canadian breakfast and brunch chain founded in 1975, specializing in Dutch-style pannekoeken, hearty omelettes, and signature DeBakon. The Tsawwassen location sits near the BC Ferries terminal, making it a natural fueling stop for cyclists arriving from or heading to the Gulf Islands and Vancouver Island. The restaurant is licensed for alcohol and offers both dine-in and delivery service through DoorDash.
