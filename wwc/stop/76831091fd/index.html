Argyle Village Pollinator Garden

Info🏛️ Public · Park / Nature📍 West VancouverFree
Bike parkingPublic rack nearby

Argyle Village Pollinator Garden is a community garden in North Vancouver dedicated to supporting pollinators such as bees and butterflies through native and flowering plantings. As a quiet green space tucked into a residential neighbourhood, it offers a pleasant stop for cyclists exploring the North Shore. The garden reflects a growing network of community-led urban greening initiatives across Metro Vancouver.
