Leopold's Tavern

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Brewery / Pub📍 Victoria$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Leopold's Tavern is a proudly Canadian-owned neighbourhood bar chain with a Victoria location, focused on classic comfort food, good beer, and community. The bar operates under an explicit safe-space policy welcoming all guests regardless of religion, race, gender, or physical ability. Kids are welcome with an adult until 8 PM, and food is available for dine-in, takeout, or delivery via major platforms.
