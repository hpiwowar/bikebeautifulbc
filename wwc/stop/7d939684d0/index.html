The Hive

Welcoming🏛️ Public · Community Centre📍 Port Coquitlam$$$
Bike parkingWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

The Hive is a multi-location indoor climbing gym chain with a Port Coquitlam outpost inside a village-style retail complex. It offers bouldering and roped climbing in a community-focused environment, making it a natural rest-day destination for cyclists touring the Tri-Cities area. Waivers are required for all visitors and can be signed online before arrival.
