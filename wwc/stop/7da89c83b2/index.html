Okanagan Falls Library Branch

Info🏛️ Public · Library📍 Okanagan FallsFree
Water bottle refillWashrooms

The Okanagan Falls Branch is part of the Okanagan Regional Library system, one of the largest public library networks in British Columbia, serving over 399,000 people across 30 branches. Located on Railway Lane in the heart of Okanagan Falls, this small branch offers public computer terminals, community programming, and a book drop accessible around the clock. It serves as a quiet, climate-controlled stop for passing cyclists looking to connect online or take a break.
