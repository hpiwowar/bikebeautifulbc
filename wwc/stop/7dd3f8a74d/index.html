Taverna Gorgona

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$$ · Upscale
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Taverna Gorgona is an authentic Greek restaurant in the heart of Ladner Village that has been operating since 1996. The menu features traditional dishes like moussaka, souvlaki, lamb chops, and charbroiled ribs alongside a wide selection of Greek appetizers and dips. A cozy, family-friendly spot well-suited for a sit-down dinner after a day riding the flat farmland routes of South Delta.
