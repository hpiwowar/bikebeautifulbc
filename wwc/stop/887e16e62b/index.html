Roots and Wings Distillery

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Winery / Cidery📍 Langley$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Roots and Wings Distillery is Langley's first craft distillery, a family-owned farm operation near Fort Langley producing seed-to-sip spirits — including vodka, whiskey, gin, absinthe, and liqueurs — from ingredients grown on their own land. Visitors can sample craft cocktails, enjoy an in-house menu, and browse a bar gift shop stocked with artisan bottles to take away. The rural Fraser Valley setting and farm-to-glass story make it a distinctive destination on cycling routes through the Langley countryside.
