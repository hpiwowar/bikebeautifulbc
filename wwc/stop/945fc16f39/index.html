Feast & Fallow

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Vancouver$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GF

Feast & Fallow is a fully plant-based/vegan café in Vancouver's Cambie corridor, serving locally sourced coffee, house-baked pastries and breads, savoury toasts, and a Saturday brunch menu. The café operates with a volunteer crew and is deeply mission-driven, advocating for a just urban food system through community workshops on topics like urban agriculture, composting, and food security. A touring cyclist stopping here will find a welcoming, community-minded space with a full espresso bar and substantial vegan food options.
