Raven's Nest Outdoor Group Campsite

InfoMunicipal · Public Campground📍 Surrey

Group-only campsite within Metro Vancouver's regional park system, designed for clubs or organized touring parties rather than solo or pair cyclists; fee applies and a group reservation is required.
