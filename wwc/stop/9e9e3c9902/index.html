Chai Coffee Company

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Chai Coffee Company is a specialty chai and coffee café founded by a Punjabi family with a three-generation-old homemade chai masala spice blend, positioned at the doorstep of Surrey's Scott Road Transit Hub. Alongside its signature chai varieties, it serves specialty coffee sourced from Canadian roasters and a food menu spanning Indian snacks like samosas and kathi rolls to croissants and freshly prepared sandwiches. Its transit-hub location and strong takeout focus make it a practical and culturally distinctive stop for cyclists passing through the Fraser Valley corridor.
