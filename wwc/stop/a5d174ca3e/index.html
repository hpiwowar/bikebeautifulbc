Salty's Beach House

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Penticton$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Salty's Beach House is a full-service waterfront restaurant on the sunny southern shore of Okanagan Lake, serving an eclectic menu that spans fresh seafood, sushi, plant-based bowls, and weekend brunch. The two-level space features an outdoor upper deck and an upstairs event lounge called The Pearl. Its prime spot on Lakeshore Drive puts it directly on one of Penticton's most popular cycling corridors.
