Surrey Art Gallery

Info🤝 Nonprofit / community · Museum / Gallery📍 SurreyFree
Water bottle refillWashrooms

Surrey Art Gallery is a public contemporary art gallery operated by the City of Surrey, presenting changing exhibitions and permanent artworks by local and national artists. It offers talks, tours, and courses alongside visual arts classes and an UrbanScreen digital art program, making it a culturally rich destination within the Surrey Arts Centre complex. The gallery is wheelchair accessible.
