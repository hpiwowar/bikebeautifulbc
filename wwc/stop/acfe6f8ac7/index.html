Skyhawk Restaurant

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutPublic rack nearby

Skyhawk Restaurant serves western and asian cuisines in Delta and offers both dine-in and takeout service. Located on Alpha Way, it serves as a dining stop for cycling tourists passing through the area. With two cuisine styles represented, the menu caters to varied tastes.
