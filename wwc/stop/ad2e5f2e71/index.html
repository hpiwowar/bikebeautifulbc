Vedder River Campground

InfoPrivate · Private Campground📍 Chilliwack
WashroomsFlush toilets

A Fraser Valley Regional District campground on the banks of the Vedder River near Chilliwack, with full amenities (flush toilets, hot showers, fire pits) and easy access to fishing, kayaking, rafting, and mountain biking; family-friendly and well-serviced but closer to suburban than wild.
