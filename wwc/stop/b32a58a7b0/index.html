Beach Grove Golf Club Lounge

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$$ · Upscale
Water bottle refillWashroomsVegetarian / vegan / GF

Beach Grove Golf Club is a private, member-owned coastal club near Boundary Bay in Tsawwassen, operating for nearly a century. The clubhouse hosts two dining venues—the Grille and the Bistro—serving chef-inspired menus using fresh, local, and sustainable ingredients. Guests are welcome for dining and private events, making the clubhouse accessible to non-members passing through the area.
