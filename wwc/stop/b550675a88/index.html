Zugba Flame Grilled Chicken

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Zugba Flame Grilled Chicken is a Filipino take-out restaurant inside the Henlong Market in Surrey, specializing in flame-grilled chicken Inasal and Filipino comfort foods like tocino, chicken mami, and hardinera. The restaurant draws on the founders' food memories from across the Philippines, offering a menu rooted in regional Filipino street food traditions. Dine-in seating is available inside the market, and the kitchen also fulfills large family and party tray orders.
