Little Bird Dim Sum + Craft Beer

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Vancouver$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

Little Bird Dim Sum + Craft Beer is a three-generation family restaurant on Kitsilano's West 4th Avenue, pairing traditional Cantonese dim sum with a curated lineup of BC craft beers and wines. The restaurant earned a Michelin Bib Gourmand recognition, tracing its roots to the family's earlier Flamingo Chinese Restaurant. Touring cyclists passing through Kitsilano will find a full sit-down dim sum experience alongside takeout pickup.
