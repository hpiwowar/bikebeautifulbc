Sutton Place Wine Merchant

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Liquor Store📍 Vancouver
Public rack nearby

Sutton Place Wine Merchant is a boutique wine, beer, and spirits shop in the heart of downtown Vancouver, with over 17 years in business and a curated selection of 1,000+ bottles sourced from local and international vineyards. The store employs certified sommeliers who offer personalized consultations and hosts regular guided tasting events, including a free weekly Friday tasting and monthly Backroom Barrel Sessions. Cyclists can purchase a bottle to go or have products delivered via Uber Eats, making it a convenient downtown stop for those wanting to bring home a local BC wine.
