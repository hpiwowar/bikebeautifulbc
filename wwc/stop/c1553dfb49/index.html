Kyoto Sushi

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFCovered bike parking

Kyoto Sushi Japanese Restaurant has been a fixture in Surrey's Newton neighbourhood since 2000, offering a broad, authentic Japanese menu spanning nigiri, sashimi, robata-grilled BBQ skewers, tempura, udon, and chef's special rolls. Its two-decade track record in a competitive culinary scene points to consistent quality, and the dine-in setting provides a proper sit-down respite for cyclists seeking a substantial meal mid-ride through Surrey.
