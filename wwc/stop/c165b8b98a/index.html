Kings Links Clubhouse

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashrooms

Kings Links Clubhouse is the restaurant at Kings Links Golf Course, a seaside par-72 championship links course in Tsawwassen recognized by Golf Canada as one of Canada's best links-style courses. The clubhouse features a patio with spectacular views of the golf course, Coast Mountains, Mount Baker, and the Gulf Islands. The restaurant is explicitly open to the public, not just members or golfers.
