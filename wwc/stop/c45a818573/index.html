Blue Parrot Coffee

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Vancouver$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutCovered bike parkingPublic rack nearby

Blue Parrot Coffee is a family-owned café operating since 1979, named after the competing bar in the Humphrey Bogart film Casablanca. The Hillcrest Community Centre location offers freshly squeezed juices, real fruit smoothies, and food made without preservatives or deep-frying, catering to active patrons. The business holds Beyond Green™ certification for its eco-friendly practices, and is one of the few cafés still serving lattes in signature clear glass mugs.
