Mineiros Brazilian Bistro

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Café / Coffee📍 Burnaby$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Mineiros Brazilian Bistro is a casual bistro in Burnaby serving Brazilian specialties including coxinha (crispy chicken-filled dough bites) and brigadeiro chocolates. Formerly operating as BrazEuro, the spot offers both pickup and delivery through online ordering. It sits on Edmonds Street and would make a distinctive stop for cyclists looking for something beyond typical café fare.
