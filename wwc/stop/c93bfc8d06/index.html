Deer Garden Signatures

WelcomingBuy BC (chain) · Restaurant📍 Richmond$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutPublic rack nearby

Deer Garden Signatures is a well-established Chinese restaurant chain in Richmond known for its signature fish soup noodles (魚湯米線) made without MSG. The Alexandra Road location (DG1) sits in the heart of Richmond's busy food street corridor. Online ordering and dine-in are both available, making it a practical meal stop on a Richmond cycling loop.
