North Burnaby Wonton House

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Burnaby$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

North Burnaby Wonton House is a Chinese restaurant specializing in wonton dishes in the North Burnaby area. It maintains an online menu for customer convenience. As a sit-down restaurant, it offers a proper meal stop for touring cyclists.
