Inchin's Bamboo Garden

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Surrey$$ · Casual

Inchin's Bamboo Garden is an Asian restaurant in Surrey offering dine-in service. Located in a commercial area on Fraser Highway, it provides meal and beverage options for cycling tourists.
