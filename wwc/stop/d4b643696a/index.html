Romers Burger Bar

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Vancouver$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Romer's is a casual West Coast burger bar that has won the Georgia Straight Golden Plate Award seven times for Vancouver's best burger, using locally sourced, hormone-free beef from Semlin Ranch in Cache Creek, BC. The River District location features a full bar with local craft beers, a patio, and weekend brunch, making it a solid food stop in a newer Vancouver neighbourhood. Founded in 2010, the brand is built around fresh, scratch-made food and active community involvement.
