Deas Island Regional Park

Welcoming🤝 Nonprofit / community · Museum / Gallery📍 DeltaFree
Bike parkingWater bottle refillWashroomsPublic rack nearby

The Inverholme Schoolhouse is a preserved one-room schoolhouse originally built in 1909 in East Ladner and physically relocated to Deas Island Regional Park in 1981, where it stands as a heritage museum within the park grounds. Situated on a tidal island in the lower Fraser River delta, it offers touring cyclists an evocative stop connecting agricultural and pioneer history of the region. The park itself lies along popular cycling corridors connecting Ladner, Delta, and the Tsawwassen area.
