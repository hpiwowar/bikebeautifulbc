Whole Foods Market

WelcomingGlobal chain · Grocery Store📍 Burnaby
Bike parkingWater bottle refillBasic groceriesHot foodWashroomsCovered bike parkingPublic rack nearby

Whole Foods Market North Burnaby is a large-format natural and organic grocery store on Lougheed Highway, stocking a full range of organic produce, sustainable seafood, and specialty foods. Beyond the main grocery floor, the store hosts multiple in-house eateries including a coffee bar, pizza counter, burrito bar, sandwich shop, sushi counter, and a cold-pressed juice bar, making it a strong resupply point for cyclists who want a hot meal alongside their grocery run. The store is wheelchair accessible and part of the Amazon Prime ecosystem for online ordering and pickup.
