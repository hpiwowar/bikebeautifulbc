Shaughnessy's Cove

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Summerland$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashrooms

Shaughnessy's Cove is a premium casual lakeside restaurant on South Lakeshore Drive in Summerland, operated by Mackenzie Hospitality Group since a full gut-and-rebuild renovation in 2021. The space carries nearly 35 years of history under the 'Cove' name and features a heated patio with lake views. It serves lunch, dinner, and weekend brunch, and accommodates walk-ins as well as reservations.
