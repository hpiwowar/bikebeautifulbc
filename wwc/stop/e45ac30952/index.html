Lakeview Motel and Suites

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Hotel / Motel📍 Osoyoos$$ · Budget
Nearby evening foodWi-Fi

A family-owned motel in Osoyoos situated directly across the street from a public beach on Canada's warmest freshwater lake, in the heart of BC's only desert region. The property offers standard motel rooms, suites, and a separate 3-bedroom vacation cottage, with a seasonal heated saltwater pool and complimentary BBQ facilities on-site. It sits walking distance from downtown Osoyoos restaurants and pubs, close to wineries, the Haynes Point campground, and the US border crossing.
