Mandarin Garden Buffet

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Chilliwack$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutWi-Fi

Mandarin Garden Buffet has been a Chilliwack-area institution since 1998, serving a wide-ranging Chinese smorgasbord buffet alongside a full take-out menu six days a week. It has earned the Chilliwack Times Reader's Choice award for Favourite Buffet, drawing diners from Chilliwack, Abbotsford, and Agassiz. A large private banquet room rounds out the offering for groups and special occasions.
