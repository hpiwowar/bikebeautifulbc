Mai's Vietnamese Restaurant

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Langley$$ · Casual
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeout

Mai's Vietnamese Restaurant is a family-owned establishment that has been serving authentic Vietnamese cuisine to the Langley community since 2008. The menu spans phở, vermicelli bowls, bánh mì, salad rolls, and family-style hot pot, with Western and Asian-inspired options available for catering. Cyclists can order for pickup or dine in at a full-service restaurant that prides itself on fresh ingredients and consistent quality.
