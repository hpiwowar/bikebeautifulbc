RiverHouse Restaurant & Pub

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Restaurant📍 Delta$$ · Casual
Water bottle refillWashrooms

RiverHouse is a family-owned restaurant, pub, and marina on the banks of the Fraser River that has served the Delta community since 1997. It features what it describes as the largest heated patios in Delta, with sweeping waterfront views and year-round sunsets alongside an active marina. Touring cyclists on River Road will find a full-service destination with a genuine sense of local character in the heart of Ladner.
