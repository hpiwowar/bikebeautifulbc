Best Western Premier Aberdeen Kamloops

InfoGlobal chain · Hotel / Motel📍 Kamloops$$$$ · Upscale
Nearby evening foodWi-Fi

A Best Western Premier hotel located just off the TransCanada Highway in Kamloops, offering views of the Thompson River Valley and surrounding mountains. The property sits close to Thompson Rivers University and key area businesses, making it a practical stop for cycle tourists passing through the interior of BC. An on-site restaurant serves all three meals.
