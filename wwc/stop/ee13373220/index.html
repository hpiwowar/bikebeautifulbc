Sunwolf Riverside Resort

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Hotel / Motel📍 Squamish$$$$ · Upscale

Sunwolf Riverside Resort offers 13 contemporary riverside cabins nestled in old-growth forest halfway between Vancouver and Whistler, making it a natural stop on the Sea-to-Sky cycling corridor. The property includes Fergie's Cafe, an on-site brunch restaurant that has won Best Breakfast from locals every year since 2015, drawing on locally sourced ingredients. The resort is family-owned, pet-friendly, and holds a Sustainable Tourism Certification.
