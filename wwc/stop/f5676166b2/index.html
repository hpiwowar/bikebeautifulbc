Convivial Café and Bakery

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Bakery / Donuts📍 Vancouver
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFPublic rack nearby

A small neighbourhood café and scratch bakery on the Vancouver seawall at Leg In Boot Square, a few minutes west of the Cambie Street Bridge. Convivial bakes everything fresh on-site throughout the day — from triple chocolate cookies and cheese-jalapeño scones to handmade artisan loaves — and serves rotating daily soups, toasted sandwiches, and espresso-based drinks. The café has a strong community focus, outdoor seating, and is a natural pit stop for cyclists riding the seawall.
