Stanley Industrial Alliance Stage

Welcoming🤝 Nonprofit / community · Museum / Gallery📍 Vancouver$$$$
Bike parkingWater bottle refillWashroomsCovered bike parkingPublic rack nearby

The Stanley Industrial Alliance Stage — better known as the Stanley Theatre — is a historic two-storey theatre at 2750 Granville Street in Vancouver, operated by the Arts Club Theatre Company. Situated in the South Granville neighbourhood, it is one of the city's landmark performing arts venues. Touring cyclists passing through the area will find it a recognizable cultural landmark along the Granville corridor.
