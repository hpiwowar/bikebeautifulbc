Doner Dude

InfoBuy BC (independent) · Fast Food / Chain📍 Richmond$ · Cheap eats
Water bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GF

Donair Dude is a BC-based Mediterranean fast food chain founded during the 2010 Winter Olympics that has grown into a franchise with multiple locations. The menu centers on donair wraps, platters, and poutine using BC-farmed meat slowly roasted in-house, alongside falafel, kofte, and various sides. A touring cyclist can expect a quick, filling meal with both meat and vegan options in a fast-casual setting.
