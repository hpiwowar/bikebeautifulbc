Chickpea

WelcomingBuy BC (independent) · Fast Food / Chain📍 Burnaby$ · Cheap eats
Bike parking or bike visibilityWater bottle refillWashroomsTakeoutVegetarian / vegan / GFCovered bike parkingPublic rack nearby

Chickpea is a fully vegan Mediterranean food operation that started as a Vancouver street food truck in 2016 before expanding to a brick-and-mortar restaurant on Main Street. Built around housemade hummus and a broad roster of plant-based Mediterranean dishes, it has grown into a local institution prized for inclusivity and wholesome ingredients. Touring cyclists with vegan, vegetarian, or gluten-free needs will find the entire menu accommodating, with online ordering available for both the truck and the restaurant.
